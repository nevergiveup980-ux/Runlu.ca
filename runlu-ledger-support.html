APP SUPPORT

RUNLU Ledger

Help for RUNLU Ledger, the personal income and expense ledger built around Entries and Sheet View.

Getting started

Use Quick Add for day-to-day recording. Open Entries to review individual records or Sheet View to see the month laid out.

Data & export

RUNLU Ledger is designed without a required bank connection. Export options are provided so your records remain portable.

Troubleshooting

If a screen does not update as expected, note the device model, iOS version, and the steps that produced the issue before contacting support.

Privacy questions

RUNLU Ledger does not require a bank connection. Final App Store privacy disclosures are kept aligned with the released build and its actual data handling.
