# RUNLU GUANSHI · 观势 · Trend Dynamics

Status: V0.1 foundation

## Purpose

GUANSHI is an open decision framework that combines:

1. evidence-based modern decision methods;
2. direct observation of real conditions and constraints;
3. traditional Chinese models of change, time, person and environment as cultural/heuristic lenses;
4. long-term recording, review and correction against real outcomes.

It is not presented as a scientifically proven fortune-telling system.

## Core rule

> If a traditional claim conflicts with reliable evidence, evidence wins.

Chinese: **任何传统理论一旦与可靠事实冲突，以事实为准。**

## Four-layer evidence structure

### 1. Science
Probability, decision theory, Bayesian updating, behavioral science, systems thinking and risk management.

### 2. Reality
Current conditions, resources, constraints, history, environment and observable behavior.

### 3. Tradition
Yijing, yin-yang, five phases, bagua, stems and branches, feng shui, divination and related systems are treated as interpretive or heuristic models unless independently supported by reliable evidence.

### 4. Validation
Predictions and recommendations should be recorded before outcomes are known. Repeated failures reduce a model's weight. Useful signals are tested further rather than declared true after a few successes.

## Nine lenses

- 易 — Change

## Decision loop

Observe → Frame → Estimate → Decide → Record → Review

Each stored decision should eventually support:

- facts available at the time;
- assumptions;
- traditional model inputs, if used;
- modern model inputs;
- alternatives considered;
- confidence / uncertainty;
- recommendation;
- actual outcome;
- later review;
- model-weight adjustment.

## Evidence labels planned for V0.2

- **Established** — supported by strong, relevant evidence.
- **Supported** — useful evidence exists but limits remain.
- **Exploratory** — plausible hypothesis or early evidence.
- **Traditional** — preserved from historical/traditional systems; scientific support not established.
- **Symbolic** — cultural/philosophical interpretation, not an empirical claim.
- **Rejected** — contradicted by reliable evidence or repeatedly failed validation.

## Boundaries

GUANSHI must not use divination or traditional symbolism as the deciding basis for high-stakes medical, legal, financial, safety or mental-health decisions. It may help users organize uncertainty and questions, but it does not replace qualified professional advice.

All four public languages — English, Simplified Chinese, French and Spanish — must be complete before a GUANSHI page is considered publishable under RUNLU's multilingual policy.
