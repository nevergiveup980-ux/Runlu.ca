MEASURE THE ROLL

Measure the outside diameter straight through the center. Core diameter is the empty tube diameter. “Effective compressed layer” is not necessarily the product's advertised thickness; it represents how much radial thickness one wound layer adds under real compression.

CALIBRATE FROM A KNOWN ROLL

Use the same outside/core diameter measurements above. Calibration converts one verified roll into an effective compressed layer value. Use it only for the same or very similar material and winding condition.
