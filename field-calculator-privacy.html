Field Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Overview

RUNLU Field Calculator 1.0 is designed as a local-first flooring calculation and planning tool. It does not require an account to use its core calculation features.

Data collection

The version described by this policy is designed not to collect personal data, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, financial information, or health information through the app.

Data stored on your device

The app may store calculator settings, saved materials, remnants, job items, and workflow progress locally on your device so that you can reuse them. This local data is not transmitted by the app as part of the local-only version 1.0 workflow.

Internet access

Core calculations are designed to work without an internet connection. If you choose to open an external RUNLU support, privacy, or marketing webpage, that webpage may receive ordinary web-request information such as IP address and browser information according to the website hosting provider's normal practices.

Third-party services and tracking

The local-only release is intended not to include advertising, third-party tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, scanning services, analytics, or other network features, this policy and the corresponding App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored calculator data by using available clear/reset controls or by deleting the app from your device.

Contact

For questions about this policy or RUNLU Field Calculator, visit the RUNLU Field Calculator Support page or the RUNLU website.
