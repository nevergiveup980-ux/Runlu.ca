# RUNLU AI SUPERMARKET — Third-Party Product Review Policy v0.1

Status: Internal draft. No third-party products approved by this document.

## Review record (required per provider)
- Product name, legal operator, official website and date verified.
- Product category, concrete user task, target audience, alternatives.
- Terms of service and privacy policy URLs; access dates.
- Trademark/logo usage permission, content licensing, API/commercial resale restrictions.
- Whether listing is editorial, affiliate, referral, authorized resale, or RUNLU-owned.
- Data collected, subprocessors, hosting regions when known, retention/deletion and user-consent implications.
- Pricing, currency, recurring charges, cancellation, refunds and API cost limits.
- Security evidence and known risks; claims independently substantiated.
- Reviewer, review date, decision, unresolved questions and next re-review date.

## Lifecycle
Candidate (internal) -> In Review (internal) -> Eligible for Editorial Listing -> Commercially Authorized (separate gate) -> Suspended/Delisted.

Editorial eligibility does NOT confer resale rights. If the review is incomplete, do not imply endorsement or approval.

## Mandatory checks before public listing
1. Verify operator and canonical URL; avoid impersonation and malicious redirects.
2. Review public descriptions and claims for accuracy; avoid copied promotional text.
3. Check name/logo permissions and never imply a formal relationship without proof.
4. Confirm affiliate program terms and prominent disclosure where applicable.
5. Identify privacy/security concerns; prohibit sending user inputs to providers in v0.1.
6. Check whether claims require special handling in health, finance, legal, children or other regulated areas.
7. Check that links, pricing statements and language versions are current and accessible.

## Rejection / suspension triggers
Unclear ownership, suspicious redirects, unsupported security claims, deceptive billing, unresolved material privacy issues, copyright/trademark objections, prohibited content, revoked partnership permission, or repeated user harm.

## Incident response
Immediately remove or disable affected listings on an approved release path, preserve evidence, assess whether users or authorities must be notified, and record the decision. Never conceal paid relationships.

## Review cadence
Review high-risk and paid listings before launch and after material provider changes. Schedule periodic re-review proportionate to risk; remove stale or unverified claims.

## Foundation restrictions
No third-party scripts, widgets, sign-in delegation, API calls, payments, upload forwarding, provider logos, or unverified external product cards. Static informational placeholders only.
