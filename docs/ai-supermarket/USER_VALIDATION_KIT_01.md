# RUNLU AI SUPERMARKET — User Validation Kit 01

Status: INTERNAL ONLY · Not launched · No participants recruited · No customer evidence collected.
Prepared: 2026-11-11. Owner approval required before public recruitment or website changes.

## Core question
Does a task-first, evidence-backed shortlist help a user choose a suitable AI tool more confidently than a conventional category directory?

## Pilot audience and safeguards
- Target 8–12 voluntary adult participants, ideally mixed roles (office staff, small-business owners, creators, students).
- Recruit directly without paid panels. Explain participation is optional and no compensation is promised.
- Do not ask for account credentials, private files, real customer data, health information or financial records.
- Record anonymous participant code, role category, consent, task outcomes and optional feedback only. No recordings by default.
- Obtain consent before note-taking. Do not publish participant quotes without explicit separate permission.
- Store notes privately, not in this public GitHub repository. Delete raw notes after the evaluation window chosen and disclosed before participation.

## Two comparable experiences (neutral ordering)
A. **Directory:** six generic task categories with plain-language descriptions, no endorsements or third-party branding.
B. **Guided shortlist:** one specific low-risk task, three hypothetical solution profiles labelled Option A/B/C. Each shows strengths, limitations, privacy caution, pricing-uncertainty notice and verification status. No real vendor rankings or invented performance claims.

Use two balanced sequences: half participants A then B, half B then A. Do not tell participants which design is RUNLU's preferred approach.

## Pilot task
“You need to turn a fictional, non-confidential two-page meeting note into a clear action-item summary. Find an appropriate kind of AI tool, identify what you would verify before using it, and explain your choice.”

Provide the same synthetic meeting note and the same task for both conditions. Do not require any live AI account, API, upload, purchase or third-party signup.

## Moderator script
1. “We are testing the page, not you. Participation is voluntary; you can stop at any time.”
2. “Please use this page to decide what kind of AI tool you would try for the fictional task. Think aloud if comfortable.”
3. “What made you trust or distrust this choice?”
4. “What would you need to check before entering a real document?”
5. “What do you think it would cost? What is still uncertain?”
6. “Which version made your decision easier, and why?”
7. “Would you return for a different task? What would make it worth returning?”
8. “Would you pay for a detailed workflow? If yes, what value would justify payment?” (Treat stated interest as weak evidence.)

## Proposed decision thresholds (pre-register, not industry benchmarks)
With 10 completed participants: >=7 complete a decision unaided in guided view; >=6 identify both privacy and pricing caveats; >=5 prefer guided view; zero serious factual or privacy issues. Record all failures. These thresholds only justify **another iteration**, not commercial launch.

If fewer than 8 complete sessions, report descriptive observations only. If the directory wins or users cannot identify caveats, redesign and retest before adding features.

## Reporting
Summarize sample size, participant-role mix, sequence balance, median time by condition, completion counts, caveat comprehension, preference, unexpected issues and limitations. No claims of statistical significance or global demand. Founder decides next gate.

## Non-goals
No provider listings, affiliate links, third-party APIs, user account creation, payments, production deployment or public recruitment in this pilot.
