## Distinguish four separate permissions
| Activity | Evidence required | Decision (Yes / No / Unknown) |
|---|---|---|
| Editorially describe a publicly available tool in RUNLU's own words | Independently verified facts, accurate claims, non-deceptive presentation | Unknown |
| Link to official provider website | Canonical URL and any applicable linking restrictions | Unknown |
| Use trademarks, logos, screenshots or copyrighted promotional materials | Explicit license, permission or documented legal basis; comply with brand guidelines | Unknown |
| Affiliate referrals / collect commissions | Applicable written program terms, eligibility and clear disclosure | Unknown |
| Resell seats, subscriptions, credits or API output under RUNLU billing | Current **explicit written commercial authorization**, permitted territory, customer obligations and contractual limits | Unknown |
| Send prompts, files or personal data to provider | Data processing purpose, consent/legal basis, security/privacy and retention assessment | Unknown |

**Important:** A public API, a paid subscription, an affiliate program, and permission to resell are different things. None automatically grants the others.

## Evidence and risk checklist
- [ ] Legal operator and domain independently confirmed.
- [ ] Product capability tested or accurately attributed to official documentation.
- [ ] No unverified performance, security, medical, financial or legal claims.
- [ ] Trademark/logo/asset permission verified, or use plain text only.
- [ ] Privacy notice, user-data processing and subprocessors reviewed.
- [ ] Data retention/deletion and cross-border processing assessed where relevant.
- [ ] Commercial/API terms explicitly assessed for resale, sublicensing and proxying.
- [ ] Pricing/currency/renewal/cancellation/refunds independently checked if mentioned.
- [ ] Affiliate compensation disclosure drafted if relevant.
- [ ] Regional consumer-protection, privacy and tax questions escalated where necessary.
- [ ] Risk owner, next review date and evidence links recorded.
