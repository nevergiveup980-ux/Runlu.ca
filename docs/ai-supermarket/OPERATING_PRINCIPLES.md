# RUNLU AI SUPERMARKET — Operating Principles v0.1

Status: Internal draft; founder approval required before public launch.
Parent governance: RUNLU_OPERATING_CHARTER.md.

## Mission
Help people find useful AI solutions to real tasks, with transparent distinctions between RUNLU-owned products, independently reviewed external tools, and authorized commercial partnerships. Quality and trust outrank catalog size.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
1. No resale, white-labeling, API proxying, or repackaging of third-party services without documented authorization.
2. No misleading endorsement, partnership, certification, trademark, logo, or affiliate claims.
3. No collection or disclosure of sensitive user information without a reviewed purpose, legal basis, privacy notice, retention and deletion controls.
4. No bypassing access restrictions, paywalls, DRM, rate limits, or provider policies.
5. No fabricated reviews, user metrics, pricing, security certifications, or earnings claims.
6. No unreviewed third-party submissions, executable embeds, scripts, or payment collection.
7. No unbounded AI usage costs, uncontrolled subscriptions, or unapproved spending.
8. No production deployment, payment/account changes, or irreversible operations without founder approval.

## Product labels
- RUNLU Owned: developed/operated by RUNLU; actual feature readiness must be verified.
- Independent Review: editorial recommendation only; not a partnership.
- Affiliate Link: disclose potential compensation near the recommendation.
- Authorized Reseller: only with current written authorization and reviewed terms.
- Under Review: internal-only; never publicly represented as approved.

## Approval gates
Gate 0: internal research and threat model.
Gate 1: static catalog prototype on isolated branch, no live third-party integration.
Gate 2: accessibility, responsive and EN/ZH/FR/ES checks; provenance and link review.
Gate 3: founder reviews draft PR and separately authorizes any public release.
Gate 4: paid/API/partner functionality requires a separate explicit decision, legal/financial review, test plan and rollback plan.

## Security & privacy
Default to outbound links to official provider websites. Do not proxy user prompts or payment data to external providers in the foundation release. Avoid third-party trackers and external scripts. Keep secrets server-side if integrations are separately approved later. Maintain incident reporting and prompt delisting.

## Financial control
No new paid services, provider contracts, commissions collection, billing changes or database migrations in v0.1. Before any later monetization, document per-use costs, abuse limits, refunds, tax handling, customer support, and worst-case exposure.

## Release policy
A PR is not deployment. No merge, no production publishing, and no public product claims until evidence-based acceptance and founder approval.
