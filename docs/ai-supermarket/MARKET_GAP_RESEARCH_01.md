## Problem hypotheses, NOT validated customer facts

H1. A user knows a concrete job but cannot confidently choose between similar AI tools.
H2. Users struggle to distinguish real total cost (free tier, limits, subscription, credits, cancellation) from marketing pricing.
H3. Users need clear data-handling and copyright/usage-rights warnings before uploading private documents or publishing AI outputs.
H4. Small businesses prefer a short, reproducible task workflow and honest trade-offs over an enormous directory.
H5. EN/ZH/FR/ES explanations may improve accessibility for some audiences, but demand and translation quality remain unverified.

## Recommended defensible wedge

**Need → 3 verified options → transparent evidence → safe next step**. Start with one low-risk task such as summarizing a non-confidential PDF or drafting a business email. Avoid health/legal/financial decision automation in the pilot. Present dated source links, pricing uncertainty, data warnings and no-paid-relationship labels. Do not claim hands-on testing without actually doing it.

## Validation plan (no new service or spend)

1. Recruit 8–12 voluntary participants from distinct user roles; obtain consent, do not collect sensitive prompts/files.
2. Give each participant one realistic low-risk task and a neutral tool-comparison page; record time to a confident decision, which evidence they used, and what was confusing.
3. Ask whether they would return, what information is missing, and whether they would pay for a structured guide. Treat stated willingness as weak evidence until behavior confirms it.
4. Compare a plain directory-list view with a three-option task guide. Keep order unbiased and disclose any relationship.
5. Set go/no-go criteria **before** testing: e.g., >=7/10 participants complete selection without help; >=6/10 correctly understand privacy/cost caveats; >=5/10 prefer task guide; no material factual or compliance errors. These are proposed internal thresholds, not market benchmarks.

## Risk register

- Competitive overlap: G2 already supports task-based evaluation and comparison.
- Stale prices and product claims: show last-verified date; avoid unverified prices.
- False endorsements or copied descriptions: write original factual summaries, plain-text product names, source citations.
- Privacy and prompt forwarding: no uploads, user accounts or third-party API calls in pilot.
- Selection bias and small sample: exploratory only; do not generalize to global demand.
- Commercial rights: editorial linking, affiliate commissions and API resale require separate reviews.

## Decision

**Proceed only to internal concept validation.** No proof of market gap or paid demand yet. No public product catalog, provider listing, resale, affiliate links or deployment changes authorized by this report.
