# RUNLU Ledger · App Store Launch Pack

Status: launch-candidate metadata for staging only. Do not publish until naming/legal clearance, real-data migration QA, device QA, privacy/support URLs, and App Store Connect packaging are complete.

## 1. Working App Store name

**RUNLU Ledger**

Why this is the current lead:
- It avoids the existing App Store product name `Life Ledger - Account Tracker`.
- It avoids using `Ledger Live`, a name historically associated with Ledger SAS.
- It keeps the distinctive RUNLU brand visible.
- It is short, pronounceable, and comfortably under Apple's 30-character app-name limit.

Important: web/App Store search is not a legal trademark clearance. Final submission still requires a last-pass naming check.

## 8. App description · English candidate

RUNLU Ledger is a simple personal income and expense ledger built around one idea: the same records should make sense in more than one way.

Use Entries when you want to see what happened transaction by transaction. Switch to Sheet View when you want the month laid out in a familiar grid. Both views work with the same underlying ledger, so you do not have to maintain two copies of your data.

QUICK TO RECORD
Start with the amount, choose a familiar item, then save. Frequently used items can rise to the top so regular entries take fewer taps.

TWO EDITABLE VIEWS
Entries gives you a clear chronological record. Sheet View gives you a month-by-month layout, including optional W1–W5 detail for recurring spending such as groceries or fuel. Edits stay traceable instead of silently rewriting history.

SEE THE MONTH AT A GLANCE
Check income, expenses, monthly balance, category spending, and simple cash-flow bars without opening a complicated analytics dashboard.

YOUR LEDGER, YOUR LEVEL OF DETAIL
Track each grocery trip separately, or work with a running monthly total. The underlying records stay in one place.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
RUNLU Ledger does not require a bank connection. The launch design focuses on user-controlled records, export, and backup rather than background financial-data collection.

PORTABLE DATA
Export your records in CSV or JSON so your ledger does not become a locked box.

RUNLU Ledger is designed for people who want more freedom than a rigid expense tracker and less work than a spreadsheet.

## 11. App icon direction

Do not place the full word RUNLU or tiny financial symbols in the icon.

Lead concept:
- dark RUNLU navy field;
- one restrained green accent;
- a geometric `RL` / ledger-sheet monogram;
- two offset panels or columns suggesting two views of one dataset;
- no coins, dollar signs, bank buildings, charts packed into the icon;
- readable at small iPhone sizes;
- strong silhouette in both light and dark App Store contexts.

The icon should feel like a serious utility, not a bank and not crypto software.

## 12. Product-page promise hierarchy

1. Know the month immediately.
2. Two views over one ledger.
3. Edit from either working view.
4. Record at your preferred level of detail.
5. Stay private and portable.
