Support

RUNLU LIST Support

RUNLU LIST is a lightweight, local-first list app for iPhone. It does not require an account or sign-in.

Before contacting support

Please include the RUNLU LIST version/build number, your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Helpful details

If the issue involves a list item, quantity, Done, Quick Picks, Memory, autocomplete, or duplicate handling, tell us the exact steps that led to the issue. Please do not send private or sensitive list content unless it is necessary to explain the problem.

Local data

RUNLU LIST stores its core list and Memory data locally on the device. The app does not require a cloud account to operate.

Contact

support@runlu.ca
