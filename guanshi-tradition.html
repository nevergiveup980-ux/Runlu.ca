Traditional Source Spine

Learning sequence · source history kept separate

Yijing → Yin–Yang → Five Phases → Bagua → Stems & Branches → Bazi → Six Lines / Meihua → Feng Shui

This is a learning and model-dependency line, not a claim of simple historical descent. These traditions developed across long periods, sometimes independently, and were repeatedly recombined.

《易经》 → 阴阳 → 五行 → 八卦 → 干支 → 八字 → 六爻 / 梅花 → 风水

这是一条学习与模型依赖线，不是简单的历史“谁生出谁”。这些传统跨越很长时期发展，有些彼此平行，后来又不断汇流、重组。

Yijing → Yin–Yang → Cinq phases → Bagua → Troncs et branches → Bazi → Six lignes / Meihua → Feng shui

Il s’agit d’une séquence d’apprentissage et de dépendance des modèles, non d’une généalogie historique simple. Ces traditions se sont développées sur de longues périodes, parfois en parallèle, puis ont été recombinées.

Yijing → Yin–Yang → Cinco fases → Bagua → Troncos y ramas → Bazi → Seis líneas / Meihua → Feng shui

Es una secuencia de aprendizaje y dependencia de modelos, no una genealogía histórica simple. Estas tradiciones se desarrollaron durante largos períodos, a veces en paralelo, y luego se recombinaron.

Old does not mean proven. Traditional does not mean false. Each claim keeps its own evidence label.

The learning line

Eight nodes, each with a different job.

T01Yijing · Change T02Yin–Yang · Relation T03Five Phases · Cycles T04Bagua · Patterns T05Stems & Branches · Time T06Bazi · Person × Time T07Six Lines / Meihua · Events T08Feng Shui · Environment
Important: stems and branches are historically very old; bagua belongs within the Yijing tradition; yin–yang and Five Phases developed through broader intellectual histories. The arrows above show how we learn and reuse concepts—not a strict chronology.
T01

Yijing · Change, position, transformation

The starting vocabulary is not 'fate' but change: states, positions, relations, timing and transitions. The core Zhouyi and later canonical commentarial layers must be distinguished when tracing ideas.

TraditionalSymbolic
T02

Yin–Yang · Relational polarity

Yin–yang is useful first as a language of contrast, alternation, activity/rest and relative balance. GUANSHI does not automatically translate it into a scientifically established physical force.

TraditionalSymbolic
T03

Five Phases · Cycles and correspondences

The Five Phases become a reusable relation model across later traditions. The Great Plan in the Shangshu is an important early textual anchor. Metaphorical relations and measurable causal claims remain separate.

Traditional
T04

Bagua · Compact pattern classes

The trigrams compress states and images into eight categories. Xi Ci and Shuo Gua are key anchors for studying how later correspondences were articulated. Classification value is not the same as proven predictive power.

TraditionalSymbolic
T05

Stems & Branches · Time scaffold

The ten stems and twelve branches form recurring calendrical combinations and the sixty-term cycle. GUANSHI separates the historical/calendar mechanism from later symbolic correspondences and predictive claims.

Historical structureTraditional interpretation
T06

Bazi · Person × calendrical time

Four Pillars combines year, month, day and hour stem–branch pairs into eight characters. The Ziping tradition is historically layered; Yuanhai Ziping, Sanming Tonghui and later commentaries must be treated as distinct textual stages rather than one timeless system.

TraditionalTest required
T07

Six Lines / Meihua · Concrete questions

These later technical traditions should not be collapsed into the ancient Zhouyi. GUANSHI will study Six-Line and Meihua methods separately, including uncertain attributions, and test any predictive output only through preregistered cases.

TraditionalExploratory
T08

Feng Shui · Environment and siting

Feng shui is split into measurable environmental variables—light, noise, airflow, access, layout, movement, terrain—and traditional symbolic rules. The Zangshu tradition is important historically, but authorship and textual history require caution.

Mixed evidenceSeparate measurable / symbolic

02

Source discipline

Four records for every traditional node.

1 · Primary textWhat the text actually says
2 · Textual historyDate, authorship, layers, later commentary
3 · Scholarly historyHow historians describe the tradition
4 · Empirical auditWhat measurable claims survive testing
A source can establish that an idea was historically taught. It cannot, by itself, establish that the idea predicts modern outcomes.
