Privacy Policy

RUNLU Warehouse OS – Flooring
Effective date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RUNLU Warehouse OS – Flooring (the “App”) handles information when you use the App. The App is designed to help users manage warehouse operations such as inventory, receiving, transfers, shipping, returns, scanning, roles, and backup workflows.

In short: RUNLU does not sell personal information, does not use third-party advertising trackers in the App, and does not receive your payment-card details from App Store subscription purchases.

1. Information the App may process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

  • Warehouse and operational data that you enter or create, such as product records, quantities, locations, receiving, transfer, shipping, return, and workflow information.
  • Account and role information when sign-in, user, or role-management features are used.
  • Backup or synchronization data when you choose to use backup, restore, or synchronization features.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the App and its supporting services.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide App functions, preserve warehouse records, support backup or synchronization features you choose to use, maintain security and reliability, troubleshoot problems, and improve the App.

3. Storage and backups

Some App data may be stored locally on your device. If you choose a backup, restore, synchronization, or hosted feature, the information required for that feature may be transmitted to and stored by the supporting service. RUNLU aims to limit processing to what is necessary for the requested feature.

4. App Store subscriptions and payments

Auto-renewable subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction. RUNLU does not receive or store your full payment-card number. Apple may provide subscription status and transaction-related information needed to confirm access to paid features. Apple’s handling of your information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.

5. Sharing of information

RUNLU does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate a feature you use, provide infrastructure, maintain security, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

6. Advertising and tracking

The App is not designed to use personal information for third-party targeted advertising. RUNLU does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through App controls, or removed when the App or its data is deleted. Information stored through optional hosted or backup features is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide those features, meet security or legal requirements, and maintain legitimate operational records.

8. Security

RUNLU uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the App and the information it processes. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

The App is intended for business and operational use and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App, its features, or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL, and the effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests related to RUNLU Warehouse OS – Flooring, please contact RUNLU through the Contact section on runlu.ca.
