# Experiment 022 — Native Task Search

## Purpose
Search for warehouse/autonomous-agent coordination tasks whose information structure is naturally nontrivial under communication outage, without importing CHSH or another Bell game first.

## Admission criteria
A candidate task must satisfy all of these before mathematical mapping:
1. Two or more autonomous decision makers have genuinely private local observations.
2. Timely communication can realistically be absent or unusable.
3. Actions must be chosen within a deadline.
4. Joint outcomes depend on the combination of local observations and actions.
5. Fixed-role and always-safe policies are meaningful baselines.
6. The operational objective is native: conflict, deadlock, delay, throughput, damage/risk proxy, or priority compliance.
7. Inputs/payoffs are not invented merely to reproduce a known Bell inequality.

## Candidate families
N1 Narrow-lane entry under local obstruction/urgency.
N2 Blind-corner merge with private stopping-distance/occupancy evidence.
N3 Dual-end handling of a long load with private local stability/clearance state.
N4 Two-zone evacuation/rerouting with locally observed blockage.
N5 Redundant machine isolation where each agent sees a different local hazard/interlock state.

## First-pass rule
For every candidate, first ask whether ordinary fixed roles, pre-agreed schedules, or a tiny classical message solve the operational problem. If yes, record that result rather than forcing a nonclassical formulation.
