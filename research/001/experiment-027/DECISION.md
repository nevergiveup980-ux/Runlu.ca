# E027 — Decision

Safety has been removed from the optimization tradeoff and treated as an invariant.

This eliminates the earlier temptation to interpret a lower stopping margin as a reason to enter.

However, the safer formulation also strengthens the classical baseline: fixed or alternating complementary roles can coordinate safe entry without communication.

Current status:
- safety invariant: retained;
- fixed-role baseline: retained;
- pre-agreed alternating schedule: mandatory comparator;
- correlation-specific advantage: not demonstrated;
- next research need: identify a genuinely native private state that changes the *efficient safe* right-of-way assignment and cannot be handled by a pre-agreed schedule.

If no such variable survives physical audit, the warehouse-native correlation hypothesis should be closed rather than embellished.
