# E094 — Shared Bias Policy

For multi-observer or multi-sensor evidence:
- record calibration/reference/training-label roots;
- compute shared-root groups before counting confirmations;
- report agreement separately from provenance dependence;
- treat observers sharing a critical root as correlated evidence for that failure mode;
- do not convert separate roots into a validity claim.

Where practical, use independently calibrated/reference-rooted measurements to test shared-bias hypotheses.

A common calibration ancestor can make many readings look precise while preserving one systematic error.
