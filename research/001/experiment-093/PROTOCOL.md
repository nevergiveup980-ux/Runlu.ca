# Experiment 093 — Measurement Reliability vs Validity

## Purpose
E092 audited the empirical measurement chain. E093 separates repeatability/agreement from correctness relative to an independently declared synthetic reference.

## Concepts
RELIABILITY: repeated measurements agree with themselves/each other.
VALIDITY: measurements agree with the independent reference quantity.

A system may be:
RELIABLE_AND_VALID
RELIABLE_BUT_BIASED
NOISY_BUT_UNBIASED
UNRELIABLE_AND_BIASED

High reliability is not evidence of validity when systematic error is possible.
