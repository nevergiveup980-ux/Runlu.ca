# E073 — Aggregation Policy

Before publishing a pooled comparison:
- preserve subgroup numerators/denominators when relevant;
- disclose subgroup weights by compared group;
- state the aggregation rule;
- test whether subgroup directions disagree with the pooled direction;
- if reversal occurs, flag AGGREGATION_REVIEW.

Do not choose pooled or stratified presentation based on which produces the preferred conclusion.

The appropriate estimand and aggregation rule must follow the research question.
