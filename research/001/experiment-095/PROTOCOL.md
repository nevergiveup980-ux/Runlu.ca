# Experiment 095 — Provenance-Cluster Evidence Census

## Purpose
E094 showed that many observers can inherit one systematic-bias root. E095 compares raw observer count with the number of declared critical provenance clusters and tests sensitivity to alternative plausible dependency partitions.

## Quantities
RAW_OBSERVER_COUNT = number of observer records.
UNIQUE_CRITICAL_ROOT_COUNT = number of distinct declared critical roots.

Neither quantity is a probability of truth or a statistically derived effective sample size.

## Partition sensitivity
The same 20 observers are audited under several declared provenance partitions:
P20: 20 separate roots.
P3: clusters of 10, 7, and 3.
P2: clusters of 17 and 3.
P1: all 20 share one root.

The exercise measures how much apparent multiplicity depends on provenance assumptions.
