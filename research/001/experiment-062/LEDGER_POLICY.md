# E062 — Claim Ledger Policy

Every headline research claim should carry:
- exact claim text;
- scope;
- applicable robustness layers;
- state per layer;
- evidence/basis per state;
- overall logical status;
- version.

Never average robustness layers.

Never let a methodological ROBUST label silently become an operationally validated claim.

A narrow claim may be robust while a broader claim containing it remains unresolved.
