# E021 — Quantum Evidence Gate

No warehouse/quantum claim may advance unless all gates below are satisfied.

G1 Native task
The task and payoff must arise from defensible warehouse/autonomous-agent semantics rather than being reverse-engineered to imitate CHSH.

G2 Information symmetry
Classical and nonclassical candidates receive the same task inputs and no hidden communication advantage.

G3 Correct classical bound
The full relevant local/randomized-classical strategy set is characterized or bounded.

G4 Operational metric
Any separation must matter to a preregistered safety/throughput objective, not merely to an abstract Bell score.

G5 Physical-resource statement
Simulation of quantum probabilities is not evidence of physical quantum advantage. A real claim requires an explicitly stated physical resource and assumptions.

G6 Robustness
The separation must survive realistic noise, loss, detector/selection effects, timing assumptions, and finite-sample uncertainty where applicable.

Until G1–G6 are met, the warehouse-native quantum lane remains exploratory.
