# Experiment 021 — Original Hypothesis Review

## Original idea
A pre-agreed correlation policy may reduce dangerous disagreement between autonomous agents with incomplete private observations when timely communication is unavailable.

## What E020 removes
If "correlation policy" means only a pre-shared classical random variable selecting among local deterministic response tables, then its expected behavior is already inside the randomized-classical convex hull. That resource cannot establish a correlation-specific advantage over the properly matched classical comparator.

## Revised decomposition
The original phrase "correlation policy" hid two scientifically different resources:

C — Classical shared correlation
A pre-shared classical random variable, common seed, lookup table, or schedule.

N — Nonclassical correlation
A resource whose observable conditional statistics cannot be reproduced by the relevant local-classical model under the stated assumptions.

E020 closes the special-advantage claim for C in the tested E004 policy class. It says nothing empirical about N.

## Decision
Do not keep the original hypothesis unchanged.

Replace it with two statements:
1. Engineering statement: shared classical coordination may still improve behavior relative to a poorly coordinated deterministic baseline, but any benefit must be compared against the full randomized-classical frontier.
2. Scientific question: does a warehouse-relevant no-communication task naturally instantiate constraints for which a nonclassical resource changes an operationally meaningful frontier?

The second statement remains an open question, not a claim.
