# Research boundary: Engineering correlation vs Bell correlation

RUNLU Research 001 now maintains two separate lanes.

**Engineering lane (E001–E005):** ordinary classical fallback coordination under partial observation and communication failure. Shared seeds/bits are classical common randomness.

**Bell sanity lane (E006):** reproduces the standard CHSH separation between local classical strategies and an ideal quantum model.

Do not transfer conclusions from the Bell lane to Warehouse OS. A future warehouse mapping must independently justify its input independence, no-communication condition, action semantics, and physical resource assumptions.
