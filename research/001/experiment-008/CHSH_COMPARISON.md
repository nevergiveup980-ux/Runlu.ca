# E008 — Native-vs-CHSH comparison plan

E008 intentionally begins with a real engineering objective: prevent simultaneous lane entry while preserving progress and respecting urgency.

A CHSH-like pattern must not be assumed.

Key diagnostic question:
Does the naturally derived preferred action relation over input pairs require a parity pattern that no deterministic local strategy can satisfy, or is the task instead solved by ordinary role assignment / priority convention?

A fixed-role policy (for example, A enters and B yields) is an important control because it can eliminate both conflict and deadlock when urgency is ignored. The urgency metric prevents us from mistaking that safe coordination convention for a complete solution.

If urgency-aware performance can be optimized with ordinary deterministic or shared-classical policies, E008 should say so. That would narrow the plausible role of Bell-style resources rather than weaken the research.
