# E078 — Dependence-Aware Uncertainty Result

E078 separates two questions that must not be conflated:

1. What is the result over a stated feasible set?
2. Why is that feasible set scientifically admissible?

The Cartesian E077-style box remains SIGN_UNRESOLVED.

A preregistered synthetic dependence relation is evaluated as a constrained feasible set. Its result is reported only as a property of that declared synthetic relation; it is not evidence that such dependence exists in a warehouse.

A deliberately invalid post-hoc filter keeps only points where D > 0.

Mathematically, that filtered set becomes ROBUST_A.

Scientifically, the constraint is rejected:
REJECT_CONSTRAINT.

This demonstrates the central danger: an analyst can manufacture apparent robustness simply by defining adverse combinations as "impossible" after seeing the result.

## Core result

Dependence-aware uncertainty can be less conservative than a Cartesian box when impossible combinations are genuinely excluded.

But dependence assumptions themselves require provenance, preregistration where possible, and independent justification.

A narrower feasible set is not automatically a better model.

## Boundary

All dependence relations in E078 are synthetic fixtures. No warehouse dependence structure has been estimated.
