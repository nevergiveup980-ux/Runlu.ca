# E074 — Standardization Policy

A standardized result must name its target population.

Do not present a standardized absolute rate without the weights that define it.

Do not choose target weights after seeing which target produces the preferred headline.

If subgroup effects differ in magnitude or direction, target sensitivity can affect the overall contrast and must be audited explicitly.

E074 deliberately retains the E073 rates and finds no direction reversal under common standardization.
