# E080 — Misspecification Geometry Policy

Every fragility margin must bind to a versioned perturbation model.

Do not compare raw thresholds from different geometries as though larger automatically means more robust.

Before comparison, check parameter meaning and units.

Where multiple perturbation geometries are scientifically plausible, report sensitivity across them rather than selecting the most favorable margin.
