# E066 — Claim Provenance Result

The declared chain begins with a SYNTHETIC technical result.

TECHNICAL RESULT:
"In this synthetic fixture, the method reduces the declared coordination-regret metric relative to the specified comparator."

The ABSTRACT retains:
- synthetic scope;
- declared metric;
- specified comparator.

Result: PASS_PROVENANCE.

The EXECUTIVE SUMMARY compresses this to:
"The method improves coordination."

It keeps the nominal SYNTHETIC tag in metadata but drops all three essential textual qualifiers.

Result: REVIEW_REQUIRED for ESSENTIAL_QUALIFIER_LOSS.

The WEBSITE CARD becomes:
"Proven coordination improvement for warehouse operations."

It both drops qualifiers and declares OPERATIONAL scope without a new evidence edge.

Result: REVIEW_REQUIRED for scope escalation and unsupported operational transfer.

## Core result

Metadata alone cannot rescue misleading public wording.

A derived claim must preserve enough visible qualification that a reasonable reader does not infer evidence broader than the source.

## Boundary

The fixture is synthetic and methodological. It does not establish any real warehouse performance improvement.
