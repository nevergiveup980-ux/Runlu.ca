# Experiment 081 — Outcome-Normalized Stress Comparison

## Purpose
E080 showed that native perturbation parameters (epsilon, rho, etc.) are not directly comparable across misspecification geometries. E081 compares stress models in a common outcome space instead.

## Common outcome quantity
For nominal contrast D0 and stressed contrast Dstress:

IMPACT = D0 - Dstress.

For a declared outcome-impact budget b, each geometry is calibrated so its worst admissible perturbation reduces D by at most b.

The audit then reports:
- nominal minimum contrast;
- declared impact budget;
- worst stressed contrast;
- sign status;
- native parameter required by each geometry.

## Principle
Compare geometries by a shared consequence in D-space, not by pretending their native parameter scales are interchangeable.
