# E017 — Research Boundary

The emerging result is architectural, not cryptographic:

**Trustworthy coordination requires explicit roots for identity/context, freshness, and ambiguous-state handling.**

This does not imply a particular authentication algorithm, key size, checksum, hardware root, or certification level.

It also does not revive a quantum claim. Nothing in E008–E017 shows that the warehouse-native coordination problem needs a Bell-style or quantum resource. Ordinary communication/state mechanisms remain sufficient in the current synthetic models.

Any future quantum lane must remain a separate scientific question.
