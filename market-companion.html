RUNLU Market Companion

A personal market research prototype built to make observation, records, and decisions more disciplined.

The project began with one real personal use case: following a single stock carefully, organizing market context, recording decisions, and learning from what happened next. It is intentionally being developed from actual use rather than from a generic feature list.

Personal prototypeIn developmentGeneral version being explored

What it is

A working companion for market observation, structured notes, trend context, and disciplined review of buy-or-wait and sell-or-hold decisions.

Where it is going

The next direction is a general version that separates reusable research logic from any one person, account, stock, or private workflow.

What stays private

Private account details, credentials, holdings, balances, keys, and personal trading records are not published as part of this project page.

Boundary

This is a research and decision-support prototype, not investment advice, a brokerage service, or an automated trading service.

Built first for one real use case. Generalized only after it earns the right to be.
