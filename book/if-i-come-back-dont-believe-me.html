Claire Mercer has spent years living with a disappearance that never stopped shaping her life. Then Ethan Vale comes back—but the man who returns is entangled in false names, buried records, and a system built to make the truth almost impossible to prove.

Love becomes inseparable from memory, identity, evidence, and survival. Every answer raises a harder question: if someone returns changed by everything they endured, do you trust the person you remember—or the person standing in front of you now?
