Claire Morgan expects a routine estate assignment at Seven Tidal Street. Instead, she finds a photograph that should not exist—one that links her late mother, a dead attorney, and Julian Cross, the man she has been seeing for seven months.

The discovery opens a trail of hidden records, manipulated evidence, inherited permissions, and old decisions made in the name of protection. The mystery becomes a question of privacy, consent, memory, trust, and the dangerous ease with which care can become control.
