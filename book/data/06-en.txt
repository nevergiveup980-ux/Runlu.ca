About a year and a half later, Zhang Yuansheng’s working life went through its first real transfer.

He moved from Shengli Oilfield Drilling Company No. 3 to Oilfield Construction Company No. 3.

Years later, he could no longer say with certainty whether the document was formally called a transfer order, a transfer notice, or something else.

The exact name did not matter.

What mattered was that it truly moved him from one work unit to another.

That also gave the title of this chapter its proper meaning: A Transfer Order.

Both companies belonged to the larger Shengli Oilfield system, but for a young employee, changing units still meant learning a new environment, new colleagues, new rules, and a different rhythm of work.

Unlike July 1994, when he had just graduated, Zhang Yuansheng was no longer a completely blank student.

About a year and a half at Drilling Company No. 3 had at least taught him what life inside a work unit was like: a task had to be received by someone, instructions had to be understood, and work had to lead to a result.

But a new unit was still a new unit.

Oilfield Construction Company No. 3 would become the most important stretch of his working life in China.

From the time he transferred there until he emigrated to Canada in May 2003, his professional life remained tied to that company.

Along the way, things more important than work itself would happen.

In 1997, he would marry.

In 1998, he would become a father.

Later still, a country that once seemed remote would slowly enter the family’s conversations.

But when the transfer first came, none of those things had yet become real.

For the moment, it was simply a change of work unit.

Young people usually understand such changes in practical terms: arrive at the new place, learn how it works, and keep doing the job well.

Only years later would he see that many important stages of a life begin in ways that look completely ordinary.

There is no soundtrack.

No one tells you to remember the day.

A sheet of paper, a reporting date, a new employee number or a new position can be enough to begin rewriting the years ahead.

From following his parents to Shengli Oilfield as a child, to going to Drilling Company No. 3 after university, and now to transferring into Oilfield Construction Company No. 3, the same word—oilfield—had already changed meaning several times in his life.

As a boy, it had been the new home his parents brought him to.

After university, it became the place where he began earning his own living.

With this transfer, it would also become the place where he built a family, became a father, and eventually prepared to leave.

Zhang Yuansheng did not know that ending yet.

He only knew what was written on the next page:

Oilfield Construction Company No. 3.
