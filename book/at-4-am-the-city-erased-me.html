At 4 A.M., the City Erased Me

凌晨四点，城市删除了我
John Zhang · RUNLU
● PUBLISHED ON APPLE BOOKS · ENGLISH + SIMPLIFIED CHINESE

At 3:57 a.m., Seattle systems architect Ethan Cole receives a timed video from his wife Nora, dead for seven years: After four o'clock, do not try to prove who you are. Minutes later, his bank, phone carrier, vehicle records, employer, and government identity begin denying that Ethan Cole exists.

The trail leads back to Equilibrium, a powerful urban prediction system, seven former custodians, and a Zeroing Protocol Ethan himself helped design. But every piece of identity he restores may also restore one of Equilibrium's dormant authority paths. To reclaim his life without rebuilding the power he once helped create, Ethan must separate prediction from permission.
