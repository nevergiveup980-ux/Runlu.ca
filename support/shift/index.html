RUNLU SHIFT Support

How to play

Drag a block along its permitted direction to move it toward its exit. Long blocks move along their long axis. A continuous straight drag counts as one move.

Commit · Trail · Echo

Commit may restrict a block’s future direction; Trail arrows affect entry into vacated cells; Echo may change another block’s allowed movement.

Undo · Restart · Hints

Use the in-game Undo control to reverse a move and Restart to begin the current level again. Availability and behavior of hints must be confirmed against the final V1.0 build.

FAQ

If a level seems blocked, try Undo or Restart. If touch controls do not respond, close and reopen the app and report the level and device details.

Contact support

For assistance, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app build, level number and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords.

RUNLU SHIFT 游戏支持

基本玩法

沿允许方向拖动方块，将目标方块移向出口。长方块只能沿长轴移动；一次连续直线拖动算一步。

Commit · Trail · Echo

Commit 可能限制方块后续移动方向；Trail 箭头限制进入已离开格子的方向；Echo 可能影响另一方块的可移动方向。

Undo · Restart · Hints

可使用游戏中的 Undo 撤销移动，或 Restart 重新开始当前关卡。提示功能是否提供及其行为，仍需以 V1.0 最终版本核实。

FAQ

如果关卡陷入僵局，请尝试撤销或重置。如果触控无响应，可重开游戏并反馈关卡及设备信息。

技术支持

联系支持时请提供 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、应用构建号、关卡编号与复现步骤。请勿发送密码。

Comment jouer

Commit · Trail · Echo
