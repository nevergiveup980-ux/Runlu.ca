RUNLU GUANSHI · V1.0 · T07

Six Lines / Meihua · The Grammar of Events & Forecasts

Question frozen · method separated · forecast scored

T07 is where GUANSHI stops discussing only structure and starts auditing explicit event forecasts.

Six Lines and Meihua are not treated as one timeless method. Their histories and casting rules stay separate; they share one modern requirement: the forecast must be frozen before the outcome exists.

到了 T07，观势不再只谈结构，而开始真正审查“具体事件预测”。

六爻和梅花不会被混成一种从古至今不变的方法。两者的历史、起卦与解释规则分别保存；它们只共享一条现代纪律：预测必须在结果出现之前冻结。

T07 est le moment où GUANSHI commence à auditer des prévisions d’événements explicites.

Les Six lignes et Meihua ne sont pas fusionnés en une méthode intemporelle. Leurs histoires restent séparées ; ils partagent une seule discipline moderne : la prévision doit être figée avant le résultat.

T07 es donde GUANSHI empieza a auditar pronósticos explícitos de eventos.

Seis líneas y Meihua no se fusionan como un método eterno. Sus historias y reglas se conservan separadas; comparten una disciplina moderna: el pronóstico debe quedar fijado antes del resultado.

A method that can explain every outcome after the fact has not yet shown predictive skill.

04

What may still survive

Decision value and predictive value are not the same thing.

Possible decision-process value

  • Force a precise question
  • Slow impulsive judgment
  • Generate alternative scenarios
  • Create a dated decision journal

What this does not prove

  • That symbols cause events
  • One hit validates the method
  • Every later rule is ancient
  • High-stakes decisions should follow divination
