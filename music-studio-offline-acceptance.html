iPhone Safari acceptance checklist (tap to expand)

On a real iPhone, complete these checks before approving the demo. This page uses a synthetic tone only.

  1. Tap Simulate generation; confirm Queued → Processing → Completed.
  2. Play the 3-second WAV, pause, and resume using Safari audio controls.
  3. Tap Download demo WAV; confirm Safari offers or saves runlu-offline-test-tone.wav in Files/Downloads.
  4. Tap Simulate failed job; confirm the download is disabled, then retry generation.
  5. Tap Simulate 403; confirm the existing audio stays available.
  6. Tap Simulate 401; confirm audio and download are cleared.
  7. Tap Reset demo; confirm Ready and no security event.
If iPhone Safari blocks a download or playback, record the iOS version and exact step; desktop Chromium passing does not prove Safari compatibility.
