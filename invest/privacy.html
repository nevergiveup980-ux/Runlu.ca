RUNLU INVEST Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · 生效日期：2026年10月17日

1. Scope

This policy describes the RUNLU INVEST web app and the iOS app currently in development. RUNLU INVEST helps individuals keep investment research notes, watchlists and decision records. It is not a brokerage or financial advisory service.

2. Information you enter

You may enter symbols, company names, research notes, decisions, watchlist entries and other financial-related notes. Please do not enter account passwords or other secrets.

3. Storage and transmission

The current web app stores user-entered records in browser local storage on the device. The native iOS prototype stores its records on the device. The currently implemented personal-record features do not require an account or automatic cloud synchronization. The website is hosted by a third-party infrastructure provider, which may process routine technical request data such as IP addresses for delivery, security and diagnostics. We do not claim that visiting the website is anonymous.

4. Backups

Web backups are exported as JSON files at your request and may be imported to replace local records after confirmation. Exported JSON backups are not encrypted. You control where those files are saved or shared. Native iOS backup and migration functionality is still being developed and should not be assumed available.

5. Sharing and third parties

The current personal-record features do not automatically transmit your notes to a brokerage or execute trades. We do not sell user-entered investment records. Your device, browser, hosting provider and any storage or sharing service you choose for backup files may process information under their own terms.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear the relevant browser/app storage, or remove the app and its local data. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove records if no independent backup exists. Contact us about any information you have voluntarily sent by email.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page if features or data practices change. Questions: hello@runlu.ca.

中文隐私说明

本政策适用于 RUNLU INVEST 网页版及正在开发的 iOS 应用。产品用于个人投资研究、观察名单及决策记录，不提供证券交易或投资顾问服务。

记录内容：用户可以自行输入股票代码、公司名称、研究笔记及决策记录。请勿录入密码或其他机密凭据。

本地存储：当前网页版使用设备浏览器本地存储；iOS 原型使用设备本地存储。现有个人记录功能不要求注册账号，也不自动进行跨设备云同步。网站托管服务商可能为页面交付、安全和诊断处理 IP 地址等常规技术请求信息。

备份：网页版允许用户主动导出及导入 JSON 备份。导出的 JSON 文件没有加密，保存与分享由用户自行控制。iOS 备份及迁移功能仍在开发验证中。

共享与删除：现有功能不会自动把研究笔记发送给券商，也不会执行交易。我们不出售用户输入的投资记录。清除浏览器数据或卸载应用可能删除本地记录，请提前保留独立备份。

联系与更新：若数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面。联系邮箱：hello@runlu.ca。
