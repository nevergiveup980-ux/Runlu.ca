FLOORING SOFTWARE FAQ

Questions from the warehouse floor.

What is flooring inventory software?

It is inventory software designed around flooring materials and workflows: carpet rolls and cut lengths, cartons, supplies, locations, receiving, transfers and transaction history.

How do you track a carpet roll after each cut?

Each cut should stay connected to its source roll. The system updates the remaining usable length while preserving roll identity, location and cut history.

Can one system track rolls and cartons?

Yes. Flooring software can manage roll goods and carton goods with different units while keeping both inside the same warehouse workflow.

How are carpet remnants tracked?

Remaining material can stay linked to the original material and roll history while its size, status and warehouse location are updated after cutting.
