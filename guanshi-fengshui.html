RUNLU GUANSHI · V1.1 · T08

Feng Shui · The Grammar of Environment

Measure place first · symbolism separate · added value tested

Place matters. The reason it matters must be demonstrated layer by layer.

GUANSHI separates measurable environmental conditions from traditional form, compass and auspiciousness rules. Building science explains what it can; symbolic claims keep their own label and must earn any added predictive or design weight.

环境确实重要；它为什么重要，必须一层一层证明。

观势把可测量的环境条件，与传统形势、罗盘、方位吉凶等规则彻底分开。能由建筑、环境与行为变量解释的，先由现实解释；剩下的象征规则保留自己的标签，只有经过独立检验才可能获得增量权重。

Le lieu compte. La raison pour laquelle il compte doit être démontrée couche par couche.

GUANSHI sépare les conditions environnementales mesurables des règles traditionnelles de forme, de boussole et d’auspice. Les affirmations symboliques doivent gagner toute valeur supplémentaire par des tests indépendants.

El lugar importa. Por qué importa debe demostrarse capa por capa.

GUANSHI separa las condiciones ambientales medibles de las reglas tradicionales de forma, brújula y auspicio. Las afirmaciones simbólicas solo ganan peso adicional mediante pruebas independientes.

Measure first. Symbolize second. Test added value last.

01

Two ledgers

Do not let environmental evidence certify symbolic rules.

Ledger A · Measurable environment

These variables belong to measurable disciplines.

  • daylight / solar exposure
  • temperature / ventilation / air quality
  • noise / privacy / circulation
  • safety / accessibility / usable space
  • terrain / drainage / moisture

Ledger B · Traditional symbolism

Historically meaningful does not automatically mean causally established.

  • qi / wind-water formulations
  • mountain-water symbolic forms
  • directional auspiciousness
  • bagua / five-phase correspondences
  • school-specific compass rules
Historical caution: attribution and textual layering require care.

02

Five working layers

A valid physical layer never automatically certifies the next symbolic layer.

1 · Site facts

Location, orientation and actual use.

2 · Measurements

Measure conditions directly where useful.

3 · Human-use outcomes

Define low-stakes outcomes before redesign.

4 · Traditional reading

Freeze the symbolic rule before results.

5 · Outcome audit

Test incremental value, not narrative elegance.
