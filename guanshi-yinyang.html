RUNLU GUANSHI · V0.5 · T02

Yin–Yang · The Grammar of Relation

Context first · relation defined · variables translated

Yin and yang are not permanent labels attached to things.

For GUANSHI they are first a way to ask how two tendencies relate, which side is rising or receding, and whether the relation changes with context or time.

阴和阳，不是贴在事物身上的永久标签。

在观势里，它首先帮助我们追问：两种倾向怎样相互作用？哪一边正在增强或减弱？换一个时间、位置、关系，它会不会改变？

Yin et yang ne sont pas des étiquettes permanentes fixées aux choses.

Pour GUANSHI, ils servent d’abord à demander comment deux tendances interagissent, laquelle croît ou décroît, et si la relation change selon le contexte ou le temps.

Yin y yang no son etiquetas permanentes pegadas a las cosas.

Para GUANSHI, primero sirven para preguntar cómo interactúan dos tendencias, cuál aumenta o disminuye y si la relación cambia con el contexto o el tiempo.

Yin–yang is a relational lens, not scientific evidence by itself.

01

The first correction

Relative, contextual, and changing — not fixed essence.

What GUANSHI keeps
  • contrast and comparison
  • context-dependent roles
  • waxing and waning over time
  • possible reversal or transformation
What GUANSHI does not assume
  • yin and yang are measurable physical substances
  • every situation must be exactly 50/50
  • a person or group has one permanent yin/yang identity
  • traditional labels automatically predict outcomes

02

Six operating relations

Turn a symbol into questions we can actually inspect.

01 · 对

Contrast

What two tendencies are meaningfully opposed or contrasted?

02 · 位

Context

Relative to whom, what, where and when?

03 · 互

Co-dependence

Does one side need the other to make sense or function?

04 · 消长

Waxing / waning

Which tendency is increasing, which is receding, and at what rate?

05 · 转

Transformation

Under what conditions could the dominant tendency reverse?

06 · 和

Dynamic coordination

The goal is not automatic equality.

03

Translation rule

No real-world weight until the pair becomes observable.

Traditional prompt

“This situation is too yang; it needs more yin.”

GUANSHI translation

Which measurable activity is excessive? What counter-function is under-supplied?

1 · Define pairName both tendencies precisely
2 · Set contextPerson, system, time, place, goal
3 · MeasureChoose observable indicators
4 · Watch changeTrend, rate, threshold, reversal
5 · Test actionRecord outcome and reweight
