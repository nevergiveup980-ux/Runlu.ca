RUNLU · PERSONAL HISTORY

Our Canadian Years

We came with twelve pieces of luggage. We began a new life, one ordinary day at a time.

On May 10, 2003, a family of three left China for Calgary, Canada. It was the mother's birthday. It was also the beginning of a new chapter, during the uncertainty of the SARS outbreak. These are their memories, told through interviews, checked against what they actually remember, and published one chapter at a time.

我们的加拿大岁月

带着十二件行李来到加拿大。往后的生活，是一天一天慢慢建立起来的。

2003年5月10日，一家三口从中国启程，目的地是加拿大卡尔加里。那天是妻子的生日，也是他们在非典疫情期间，带着期待与不安踏上移民之路的日子。这个系列采用访谈方式，记录一家人的真实回忆。记得清楚的认真写，尚未确认的不擅自补写。
