## Verified external references

1. **BDC — 7 steps to setting the right price for your products or services** (updated 2023-08-01)
   https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/marketing-sales-export/marketing/steps-setting-right-price-your-products-services
   Supports: direct labour/material costs, additional delivery costs, indirect overhead, markup calculation, margin definition, and the need to revisit pricing. BDC explicitly describes travel, equipment maintenance and accounting costs as potential sources of underpricing.

2. **BDC — What are general expenses?**
   https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/entrepreneur-toolkit/templates-business-guides/glossary/general-expenses
   Supports: overhead as indirect operating costs and distinction between general expenses and costs of sales. Do not represent our fictional $100 allocation as a BDC recommendation.

3. **BDC — What are direct costs?**
   https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/entrepreneur-toolkit/templates-business-guides/glossary/direct-costs
   Supports: tracking direct costs as a component of pricing and profitability analysis.

4. **BDC — The 5 most common pricing strategies**
   https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/marketing-sales-export/marketing/pricing-5-common-strategies
   Supports: cost-plus as only one pricing method; market willingness to pay and competitive positioning matter too.

5. **BDC — Pricing strategy in uncertain times**
   https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/business-strategy-planning/manage-business/what-pricing-strategy-should-you-adopt-in-uncertain-times
   Supports: evaluating customer value and checking existing service agreements when considering price changes. Does not establish legal entitlement to change a particular contract.
