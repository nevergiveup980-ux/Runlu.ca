One Long Weekend, Three Milestones

A promise given a shape

One book

One game

One connection

Any candidates found by the prototype require further research and human review before a decision. Connection success does not establish automated trading capability. Private account details and credentials are not part of this public record.

What remains after the weekend

A milestone is useful when it becomes a foundation for the next piece of work. A book needs readers, a game needs players, and a research tool needs repeated checks. Gratitude here is also practical: appreciating what has been built enough to keep caring for it.
