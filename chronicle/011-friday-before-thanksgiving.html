The Friday Before Thanksgiving

Some traditions begin quietly. Since 2023, when Wood’s Homes awarded GIMEX a cleaning contract and a direct working relationship began, GIMEX has made an annual CAD $1,000 donation to Wood’s Homes through its Foundation Department on the Friday before Canadian Thanksgiving. Four years have brought the total cash contribution to CAD $4,000.

That Friday is deliberately taken off work, setting aside a day for this commitment. The contribution supports the organization’s work with children, young people and families who need care and assistance.

In both 2025 and 2026, about ten original RUNLU hats were also given each year to Wood’s Homes staff, mainly in the first-floor offices. In 2026, four large coffee servings and four boxes of doughnuts were shared as well. These are gifts to staff, distinct from the formal GIMEX donation.

The meaning is not in the size of any single gesture. It is in returning, year after year, and keeping a small promise to the community. This is a personal record of giving, not an announcement of a formal partnership or endorsement.
