RUNLU’s First English Novel Goes Live on Google Play

On November 2, 2026, RUNLU submitted THE GLASS THRONE: A Corporate Suspense Novel, by John Zhang, to Google Play Books. After an initial processing error and a cover-quality warning, the EPUB was processed successfully and a replacement cover was received.

Later that day, Google’s book status confirmed: “Your book is live on Google Play and Google Books.” The title is available for sale in the regions listed by Google, including Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom, and is previewable on Google Books. Its Canadian list price was CA$6.99.

With three Chinese-language titles already live on Google Play, THE GLASS THRONE became RUNLU’s first English-language title on the platform—a milestone in reaching English-language readers.

Milestone confirmed November 2, 2026: live on Google Play and Google Books.
