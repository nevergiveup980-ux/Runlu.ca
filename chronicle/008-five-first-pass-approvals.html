RUNLU CHRONICLE · OCTOBER 19, 2026

Five Times, First Pass.

On October 19, Apple completed its review of RUNLU INVEST 1.0. The submission was eligible for distribution. For RUNLU, it marked a fifth consecutive app approval on the first submission.

The app had been submitted the previous day. Before that, there were ordinary but stubborn hurdles: selecting the correct distribution build, completing App Store metadata, and providing the privacy policy link for the Simplified Chinese locale. Each small obstacle had to be solved before the review could begin.

RUNLU INVEST is deliberately modest in its first version: a place to keep investment observations and research notes. Passing review does not prove that a market exists. It does mean that another idea has become a real, reviewable piece of software.

Five first-pass approvals are not a promise that the sixth will be easy. They are a reminder that careful work, repeated patiently, can become a reliable practice.

CHRONIQUE RUNLU · 19 OCTOBRE 2026

Cinq approbations dès la première soumission.

Le 19 octobre 2026, Apple a terminé l'examen de RUNLU INVEST 1.0 et confirmé son admissibilité à la distribution. Il s'agissait de la cinquième approbation consécutive de RUNLU dès la première soumission.

Soumise la veille, l'application avait d'abord nécessité plusieurs corrections : une version de distribution appropriée, les informations de l'App Store et l'URL de confidentialité pour le chinois simplifié.

Cette première version reste volontairement simple : un espace pour les observations et les notes de recherche sur l'investissement. L'approbation ne garantit pas un marché, mais marque la transformation d'une idée en logiciel concret.

Cinq réussites ne garantissent pas la sixième. Elles rappellent la valeur de la patience et du soin apporté aux détails.
