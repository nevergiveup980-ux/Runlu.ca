THE ORIGIN · A DEVELOPER'S NOTE

A system begins with the work itself.

Years of everyday warehouse work made one problem impossible to ignore: information often has to be entered again when work moves from one person or department to another. The first impulse was not to build a product. It was to make the next handoff clearer.

Flooring Business OS is still a working build. Its ambition is to connect the wider workflow without pretending every integration is already finished.

01 / Match the request

Match carpet name, colour and requested area in SF or SYD against eligible inventory candidates.

02 / Dyelot-first selection

Check dye-lot consistency first, then favour the smallest suitable roll or the closest practical size to reduce waste.

03 / Cut-by-roll proposals

When cut dimensions are provided, propose which cuts come from which roll and estimate the remaining usable material.

04 / Roll-level visibility

Surface Deerfoot / warehouse and manufacturer roll IDs, dyelot, width, usable length and estimated remainder.

05 / Two PO workflows

Designed for both digital purchase orders and sales entering information from traditional paper POs.

06 / Human-verified execution

Sales reviews and confirms the recommendation; the warehouse performs the final physical and cutting verification.
