CONNECTED WORKFLOW

Departments change. The job record continues.

The operating idea

A flooring job should not restart every time it moves from sales to purchasing, from purchasing to the warehouse, or from completed work to invoicing. Each handoff should carry forward the information already earned.

Why it was built

The system grew from repeated work, missing handoffs, duplicated entry, and small errors that become expensive when departments cannot see the same operational truth.
