RUNLU STUDIO · 03

LINGGUANG
Health OS

A private-practice workflow prototype shaped around acupuncture and Chinese medicine — bringing patient intake, practitioner work, appointment flow, and careful technology into one calmer system.

Practice workflow prototype English-first build · In development

The core paths and interface direction have been established. Authentication, form behavior, scheduling, and voice interaction continue to be refined and tested.

ONE PRACTICE · TWO CLEAR PATHS

Designed around the people who actually use it.

01

Patient path

A focused entry for account access, intake, appointment requests, and the information a patient needs to provide.

02

Practitioner path

A separate working area for reviewing submitted information, managing the day, and supporting the practice workflow.

03

Structured intake

A detailed questionnaire engine designed to make a long intake process clearer, easier to continue, and more useful to review.

04

Appointments & calendar

Request-based and scheduled appointment paths, with day, week, and month views being shaped for practical use.

05

Local voice tools

Local commands are explored for simple navigation and actions where speed and privacy matter more than open-ended conversation.

06

AI-assisted experiment

A parallel GPT-assisted voice path is being tested as an optional layer. It remains experimental and does not make clinical decisions.

BUILT FROM A REAL PRACTICE NEED

Technology should reduce friction, not interrupt care.

Why it began

The project began with a real challenge: a long and detailed intake process, different needs for patients and practitioners, and the desire to keep appointments and information from becoming scattered across unrelated tools.

How it is being shaped

The system is refined through repeated interface tests: login flow, return navigation, form continuity, microphone behavior, spoken responses, and whether each screen feels understandable on a phone.

PRIVACY & SCOPE

A public project page with a deliberately private interior.

What stays private

No patient record, questionnaire response, appointment detail, credential, clinic account, or private application entry is published through this portfolio page.

What the system is not

LINGGUANG Health OS is a workflow prototype. It is not presented as a diagnostic tool or medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not replace the judgment of a qualified practitioner.

Built to support the practice around care — while leaving care itself in human hands.
