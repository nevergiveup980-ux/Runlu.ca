Privacy

RUNLU TWIST 1.0 · 2026-10-24

The app works offline. Progress, hints, self-assessment and language preference are stored only on your device. RUNLU TWIST has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not send gameplay data to RUNLU.

Sharing sends only the result text you choose to a destination you select in the iOS share sheet. That destination’s privacy policy applies.

Reset progress clears puzzle progress; language preference remains. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
