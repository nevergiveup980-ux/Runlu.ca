PRIVACY POLICY

Universal Invoice Privacy

This page describes the intended privacy approach for RUNLU Universal Invoice. The final App Store privacy disclosure should always match the behavior of the exact app version being submitted.

Our approach

Universal Invoice is designed around a focused invoicing workflow. We aim to collect and process only information that is necessary to provide the app's functions, support purchases, and respond to support requests.

Invoice information

Invoice and business information you enter into the app is used to create and manage invoices. Where the app stores information locally on your device, that information remains subject to your device and backup settings. Features that use cloud or external services should be disclosed in the release that enables them.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription restoration are handled through Apple's StoreKit and App Store systems. RUNLU does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Export and sharing

When you choose to export, share, or email an invoice, the information included in that document is provided to the destination or service you select. You control when these actions occur.

Diagnostics and support

If you contact support, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as app version, device details, screenshots, or a description of the issue.

Data selling and advertising

Universal Invoice is not intended to sell personal information or use invoice content for third-party advertising.

Apple privacy disclosures

The iOS application includes an Apple privacy manifest as required by the app build. App Store privacy answers should be reviewed against the exact shipping version before release.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@runlu.ca.
