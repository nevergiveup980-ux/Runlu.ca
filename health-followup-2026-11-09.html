From 70 kg to 63.5 kg

Personal practice · 9 November 2026

70 kgJune 2026
63.5 kg9 Nov 2026
−6.5 kg−9.3%

Around June 2026, my weight was approximately 70 kg. On 9 November 2026, it was 63.5 kg—a decrease of 6.5 kg (about 9.3%) over a little more than five months.

I gradually reduced portion sizes, added sugar and excessive fruit portions. I generally ate three meals within an eight-hour window and continued everyday activity and moderate exercise. I did not follow an extreme diet.

My subjective experience is that daily life feels much the same. That observation does not establish that the weight change improved my health or that time-restricted eating caused the change.

The next priority is maintaining adequate nutrition, strength and a stable weight rather than seeking further weight loss. Given the magnitude of the change, it is sensible to mention it at a routine medical appointment. This is a personal record, not medical advice.
