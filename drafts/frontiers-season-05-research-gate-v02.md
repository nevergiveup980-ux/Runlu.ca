# RUNLU FRONTIERS — Season V Topic Research Gate v0.2
Date: 2026-11-12
Status: Research only. Not approved for writing or publication.
Provisional series title: At the Crossroads of Civilization / 当文明面临新的选择

## Editorial thesis
Season IV asked how humanity might redesign the future. Season V asks how changing technologies, institutions, and relationships may change human identity, autonomy, trust, and the conditions of shared life. Distinguish empirical claims from speculation and philosophical argument.

## Evidence checked (primary institutions)
1. UNESCO, 2025 Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology, adopted November 2025. Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/node/86248
2. UNESCO explanation of neurotechnology, mental privacy and autonomy: https://www.unesco.org/en/ethics-neurotech
3. OECD Survey on Drivers of Trust in Public Institutions 2026 Results: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-survey-on-drivers-of-trust-in-public-institutions-2026-results_9eb63fec-en.html
OECD 2026 reports 40% high/moderately high trust in national government, 43% low/no trust across participating OECD countries; these figures should not be represented as global population figures.

## Ten candidate topics — preliminary editorial decisions
01 When Thoughts Become Data / 当思想成为可以读取的数据
Status: Priority. Specific focus on neural data, inference limits, consent, mental privacy, cognitive liberty. Do not imply technology can read any thought accurately. Distinguish from Season IV general privacy chapter.
02 What Is Human Consciousness? / 人类的自我意识究竟是什么？
Status: Conditional. Compare competing scientific theories and unresolved questions; never claim the hard problem has been solved.
03 When Reality Becomes Hard to Recognize / 当真实越来越难以辨认
Status: Priority for further evidence. Focus on provenance, verification, institutional trust and the limits of media literacy, not generic AI risk.
04 Can Society Survive Without Trust? / 一个失去信任的社会还能运转吗？
Status: Priority. Anchor in OECD survey and distinguish trust in national government from interpersonal trust.
05 What If Memories Could Be Changed? / 如果记忆可以被修改
Status: Conditional. Separate established memory reconsolidation research from speculative memory editing and science fiction.
06 When Humans Bond with Machines / 当人与机器建立情感关系
Status: Conditional. Focus on reciprocity, dependency, disclosure and safeguards; avoid conflating conversational simulation with consciousness.
07 Why Do Civilizations Become Fragile? / 文明为何会变得脆弱？
Status: Priority for evidence. Examine infrastructure, supply chains, institutional failure and resilience; avoid single-cause collapse stories.
08 Can Science Explain the Meaning of Life? / 科学能否回答人生的意义？
Status: Conditional. Clearly distinguish descriptive science from philosophical and personal values.
09 When Humanity Redefines Nature / 当人类重新定义自然
Status: Needs re-scope. Risk of overlap with Season IV planetary stewardship. Explore ecosystem intervention, restoration, unintended consequences.
10 Civilization's Next Awakening / 文明的下一次觉醒
Status: Needs retitle. 'Awakening' is metaphorical and risks overclaiming. Finale should integrate responsibility, knowledge, freedom and pluralism without prophecy.
