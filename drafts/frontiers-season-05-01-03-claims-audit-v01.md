# RUNLU Frontiers V — Cross-essay claims audit v0.1
Date: 2026-11-14
Status: internal research; not approved for drafting or publication.

## Purpose
Avoid conceptual repetition and prevent unsupported claims in proposed essays 01 (neural data) and 03 (digital provenance).

## Shared principle
An inference is not direct access to the underlying reality.
Essay 01: neural measurements -> model inference about mental activity. Central questions: consent, mental autonomy, validity, privacy.
Essay 03: digital artifact -> provenance/integrity evidence -> assessment of event claims. Central questions: authenticity, corroboration, verification, institutional trust.
These are different mechanisms, harms and governance responses. Avoid repeating generic warnings about AI.

## Source checks performed 2026-11-14
UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology, adopted 2025-11-11:
https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-neurotechnology
Specific passages: 43–50 (autonomy, informed consent, neural and inferred mental-state data); 125–130 (workplace). This is a recommendation to member states, not automatically enforceable domestic law.

NIST AI 100-4 (2024-11-20), Reducing Risks Posed by Synthetic Content:
https://www.nist.gov/publications/reducing-risks-posed-synthetic-content-overview-technical-approaches-digital-content
Official PDF: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-4.pdf
Topics: content provenance, watermarking, detection, testing and auditing. Do not assert any technique proves event truth.

## Preliminary claim ledger
01-A Supported: UNESCO recommends protection for freedom of thought, mental privacy and informed consent.
01-B Unsupported: current systems can accurately read anyone's thoughts without cooperation.
01-C Requires review: scope of consumer neurotechnology in Canada, clinical BCI evidence, Canadian law.
03-A Supported: NIST surveys technical methods for provenance, watermarking and synthetic-content detection.
03-B Inference to label: provenance can help evaluate origin, but origin alone does not establish factual truth.
03-C Unsupported: absence of a watermark proves a file is authentic.
03-D Requires review: real-world court, newsroom and platform examples; C2PA effectiveness and metadata failure modes.
