# RUNLU FRONTIERS V — Essay 06 Official Offboarding Documents Audit v0.8
Date: 2026-11-16
Status: INTERNAL EVIDENCE REVIEW — not publication clearance.

## Purpose
Test the distinct thesis: ending a chat, cancelling recurring billing, deleting an account and deleting conversation history are different actions. Official support and legal documents are evidence of stated procedures, **not direct tests of the live interface** and **not proof of manipulative intent**.

## Product 1: Replika
Official cancellation guide: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032500012-How-to-cancel-your-subscription
- Deleting app does not cancel subscription; subscription must be cancelled on originating platform. iOS subscriptions through Apple, Android through Google, other methods vary.
Official account deletion guide: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001095932-How-do-I-delete-my-account
- Deletion is irreversible and described as removing profile, history, photos and exchanged content.
Official post-deletion billing FAQ: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37121179315469-Why-did-I-get-billed-after-deleting-my-account
- Explicitly warns account deletion does not automatically cancel app-store subscription.
Official conversation deletion FAQ: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410750548493-Can-I-delete-my-conversations
- States there is no way to delete all message history without deleting account.
Official privacy policy: https://replika.com/legal/privacy/en
- Describes retention, including deletion within 60 days for some account information, and longer legal/accounting retention for financial records; **do not equate user-facing 'permanent deletion' with immediate eradication of every record**.
Official current terms: https://replika.com/legal/terms/en
- Different rules for web and app-store subscriptions; confirm platform-specific terms rather than flattening all into one policy.

## Product 2: Character.AI
Official account deletion guide: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/15063461160475-How-do-I-delete-my-account
- Web: Profile > Profile Settings > Account > Manage Account & Data > Delete Account. Mobile: Profile > Settings > Remove Account. Permanent, irreversible.
Official subscription cancellation guide: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/17399592326555-How-do-I-manage-or-cancel-my-c-ai-subscription
- Web subscription managed through Customer Portal (Settings > Account > Manage); app subscriptions through Apple/Google.
Official lite plan FAQ: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/55388952900379--c-ai-lite-FAQ
- For web lite subscriptions, support ticket may be needed; distinguish from c.ai+ portal guidance. Policies can differ by product tier.
