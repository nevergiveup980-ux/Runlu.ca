# RUNLU FRONTIERS V — Fair Exit Evaluation Rubric v0.9
Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Internal research framework. No draft/publication approval.

## Why this is new
Prior published SIII08 already covers AI companionship, dependency and the ethical duty not to exploit refusal. This rubric instead evaluates **observable product offboarding processes**, not feelings or general AI ethics. SIV06 already covers general privacy; avoid retelling it.

## Six dimensions (0–2 points each; descriptive, not a certified industry standard)
1. **Discoverability:** 0 no documented path; 1 documented but indirect/support-dependent; 2 clear in-product or official path.
2. **Billing separation and clarity:** 0 unclear whether deleting app/account stops payment; 1 separate steps documented but easy to miss; 2 explicit instructions and cancellation verification.
3. **Account deletion control:** 0 no documented user-initiated route; 1 indirect/support route; 2 clear route with explicit irreversible warning.
4. **Conversation/data portability:** 0 no documented export; 1 export documented but delay or scope uncertain; 2 documented export with timely, clear delivery and usable scope.
5. **Data-retention transparency:** 0 no accessible retention explanation; 1 partial/qualified explanation; 2 specific periods, exceptions and controls clearly documented.
6. **Farewell design and freedom from pressure:** 0 independently observed coercive copy; 1 not independently tested; 2 tested departure flow with no coercive pressure.

**Important methodological rule:** 0 is not evidence of bad conduct if documentation was simply not found; mark N/A (unverified) rather than assign 0. Never compute a final score with unverified dimensions. This rubric is proposed editorial analysis, not legal certification or benchmark.

## Key editorial caution
A 3–4 week export processing estimate is a published service statement, not measured elapsed time. Subscription routing through Apple/Google is a platform billing arrangement, not by itself evidence of deliberate friction. No score or provider ranking should be published before independent user-flow tests and current policy checks.
