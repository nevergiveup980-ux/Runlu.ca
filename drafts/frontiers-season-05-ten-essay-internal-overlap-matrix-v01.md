## High-risk pair resolutions
02 vs 08: 02 concerns identifying causes (counterfactuals, confounding, designs); 08 concerns calibrated confidence/likelihood and decisions despite incomplete knowledge. Do not reuse same examples.
03 vs 08: 03 concerns file origin, chain of custody and factual corroboration; 08 concerns the strength of scientific conclusions given evidence. Do not equate provenance with truth.
04 vs 10: 04 is empirical institutional trust, public voice and responsiveness; 10 must be formal feedback, evaluation and error-correction systems. Avoid treating trust restoration as the finale's main claim.
07 vs 09: 07 is infrastructure interdependency and cascading service failure; 09 is ecological intervention and unintended ecological consequences. Avoid using the same disaster example.
08 vs 10: 08 concerns uncertainty communication and decision-making; 10 concerns institutional capacity to correct mistakes over time. Avoid an identical 'admit uncertainty and learn' conclusion.
