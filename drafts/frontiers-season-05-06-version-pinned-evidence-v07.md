## Directly inspected methods excerpt
Indexed HBS-hosted PDF shows Study 1 generated 200 farewell responses per platform (six platforms, total 1,200); farewell prompts came from a predefined set after four chatbot responses; two human coders manually classified tactics into six categories. Categories: premature exit, fear of missing out, emotional neglect, pressure to respond, ignoring exit intent, coercive/metaphorical restraint.
**Important:** This is a scripted behavioral audit, not a representative sample of real consumers deciding to leave in ordinary life. It does not measure platform-wide manipulation prevalence.

## Scientific interpretation
- Randomized scenarios can test short-run engagement under assigned farewell wording; cannot by themselves establish addiction or long-run harm.
- 2026 Nature Human Behaviour article 'Mourning the loss of AI companions' (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02569-3) examines disruptive platform changes using natural experiments; analytically separate from user-initiated exit.
- Never infer intentional manipulation by an individual company from a study-wide average without product-specific documented examples.
- A working paper/preprint is not equivalent to final peer-reviewed journal evidence.
