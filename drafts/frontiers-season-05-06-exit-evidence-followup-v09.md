## Independent verification of existing v08

### Key distinction: deleting an account versus canceling renewal
Replika's current English terms distinguish app-store billing (must cancel via Apple/Google) from web subscriptions. The help center's general warning that deletion does not cancel a subscription must not be generalized to all web subscriptions: the terms say web-account deletion also cancels the web subscription at the end of the paid period. The safest actionable advice is to check the actual billing channel and confirm cancellation there.

### Key distinction: deletion from UI versus back-end retention
The help page describes irreversible account deletion; privacy policy describes deletion of certain profile/memory records within 60 days and exceptions for legally required records. Thus 'permanent deletion' in UI does not establish immediate destruction of every retained record.

### Unverified comparator
Character.AI official help documents account deletion and separate subscription management. These pages alone do not establish its full conversation-export, partial-delete or data-retention rules. No claim about these is cleared.

## Editorial decision
The strongest new contribution is the *four-layer exit model*: stop interaction, stop billing, delete account, and manage data. This does not prove emotional manipulation. The new essay must distinguish vendor-described workflows from experimentally measured farewell messages, and never imply the research experimentally tested these two vendors' cancellation interfaces.
