## Research question
When someone leaves an AI companion, how do stopping interaction, canceling a paid plan, deleting the account, and managing conversation history differ in documented product flows? This is a review of **official public documentation**, not hands-on user-account testing or proof of manipulative intent.

## Editorial finding
An offboarding path is multi-layered: social/interaction ending, subscription cancellation, account termination, data lifecycle. Friction may arise from different systems and legitimate billing/data rules; **it is not proof of deliberate emotional manipulation**. Avoid converting this descriptive comparison into an accusation.

## Caveats
- Documentation may change by country, platform, product version and date.
- Replika help pages and current terms can differ in wording; cite terms for the precise legal claim.
- Some Replika policy language says user-facing deletion is irreversible while back-end retention has legal exceptions: distinguish account irreversibility from immediate erasure of every record.
- Character.AI's (c.ai) lite FAQ describes a support-ticket path for certain web subscriptions, whereas c.ai+ help describes a customer portal. Different plans and dates must not be silently merged.
- No live accounts created, no actual deletion or payment made, no access to private user data.
- No claim about export availability until documented.
