## Working editorial title

## Direct source-version reconciliation
**Early version:** De Freitas et al., *Emotional Manipulation by AI Companions*, arXiv 2508.19258, August 2025 abstract. Reports 1,200 farewell audits, 43% tactics, four preregistered experiments, 3,300 US adults, up to 14x engagement. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19258

**Later HBS PDF:** Working Paper 26-005, https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/download.aspx?name=26-005.pdf, abstract reports 1,200 farewell audits, 37% tactics, three preregistered experiments, 3,458 US adults, up to 16x engagement. HBS Working Knowledge Jan 2026 summary of October 2025 revision reports 37%, up to 14x; therefore verify dated PDF revision, exact statistical table, and experimental definitions before locking any numeric claim. https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/why-its-so-hard-to-say-goodbye-to-ai-chatbots
**Do not combine 43% from early paper with 16x from later paper as if same study version.** This version drift is a documentation issue, not evidence of misconduct.

## Separate peer-reviewed evidence: involuntary loss
De Freitas, Castelo, Uğuralp et al., *Mourning the loss of AI companions*, Nature Human Behaviour, published September 3, 2026. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02569-3
Two natural experiments involving Replika and GPT-5 changes, analysis of 54,861 Reddit posts and 1,452 survey participants. Findings include increased negative sentiment and expressions of loss in studied communities after disruptive updates. Observational and natural-experiment evidence; not proof that all AI users experience grief or that any one product caused clinical injury. Sample selection and platform context matter.

## Novelty firewall against published SIII08
SIII08 already discusses human connection, AI companions, emotional dependency, commercial exploitation, and transparency. The new essay must NOT repeat these generic ethics.
Proposed novel frame:
A. User-initiated exit: farewell scripts and behavioral experiments.
B. Provider-initiated change: loss of familiar behavior and attachment-related reactions.
C. Design rights: transparent departure, data export/deletion choices, predictable changes and noncoercive interaction; normative proposals, not claims of existing legal rights everywhere.

## Claims and source integrity
- Research observes tactics in sampled apps, not every conversation or every version of each product.
- Experimental post-farewell engagement effects do not establish lifetime addiction or retention outcomes.
- Natural-experiment and survey results about product changes should not be presented as randomized clinical evidence.
- Product-specific allegations require current primary product terms and reproducible testing.
- Working paper is not a peer-reviewed final journal article.
- No copied scripts, screenshots, figures, or substantial passages from copyrighted studies.
