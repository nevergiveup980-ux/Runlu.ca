## Research question
When someone wants to leave an AI companion, what are the distinct mechanisms for stopping conversation, canceling billing, deleting an account, and erasing conversation data?

## Evidence: official provider documentation (read 2026-11-16)
### Replika
1. Delete account: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001095932-How-do-I-delete-my-account
   Official instructions: app Settings > Account Settings > Delete Replika; web Account Settings > Delete my Account. Describes permanent removal of profile, badges, XP, chat history, photos and shared links. **Provider-stated workflow, not independent testing.**
2. Cancel subscription: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032500012-How-to-cancel-your-subscription
   Deleting app does not cancel subscription; subscriptions must be canceled via the platform used for purchase. App-store subscriptions managed in Apple/Google account settings.
3. Billing after deletion: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37121179315469-Why-did-I-get-billed-after-deleting-my-account
   Provider explicitly says deleting account does not automatically cancel an app-store subscription.
4. Conversation deletion: https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410750548493-Can-I-delete-my-conversations
   Says no way to delete entire message history without deleting account. Do not confuse selective memory editing with clearing conversation history.
5. Privacy policy: https://replika.com/legal/privacy/en
   Current public policy describes retention of ordinary text conversation history for 24 months while account open, media for 12 months, and deletion of account profile/memories/customizations/purchased-item data within 60 days of closure, subject to stated legal exceptions and categories. **Do not generalize that all data disappears instantly.**
6. Terms: https://replika.com/legal/terms/en
   Current public terms distinguish app-store and web subscriptions; web account deletion cancels web subscription, while app-store subscriptions require separate cancellation. Important distinction: provider's FAQ may summarize less precisely than current legal terms.

### Character.AI
1. Delete account: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/15063461160475-How-do-I-delete-my-account
   Web: Profile > Profile Settings > Account > Manage Account & Data > Delete Account. Mobile: Profile > Settings > Remove Account. Permanent according to provider.
2. Cancel c.ai+: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/17399592326555-How-do-I-manage-or-cancel-my-c-ai-subscription
   Web: Account > Manage > Customer Portal; app subscriptions through app store. **Official guidance, not hands-on verified.**
3. Newer lite FAQ: https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/55388952900379--c-ai-lite-FAQ
   For c.ai lite, web Stripe subscribers are directed to support ticket for payments/purchases. Different product tiers and document dates matter; avoid asserting a universal Character.AI cancellation path.

## Editorial finding
Four distinct actions: (A) stop interacting, (B) cancel future charges, (C) delete account, (D) erase conversation data. They need not happen together. Provider rules, account screens, app-store billing, and retention policies may differ. This distinction creates a strong original essay angle not covered by generic human-relationship ethics.

## Limitations and cautions
- No accounts were created, no payments made, and no real app UX or farewell dialogues were tested.
- No claim that either provider deliberately traps users, manipulates them emotionally, or violates consumer law.
- Documentation can change; cite access date and current policy edition before publishing.
- 'Data deletion' can have retention exceptions; distinguish UI visibility, deletion scheduling and legal retention.
- Cross-product comparison is descriptive, not a quality ranking or claim of legal noncompliance.
