# RUNLU FRONTIERS V — Evidence-led outline v0.3
Date: 2026-11-12
Status: Editorial research / NOT approved for writing or publication
Working title: 当文明面临新的选择 / At the Crossroads of Civilization

## Core distinction from Season IV
Season IV: how society designs its future (AI direction, work, education, privacy, machine decisions, fairness, planetary stewardship).
Season V: how individuals and institutions maintain agency, knowledge, meaning and trust when the boundaries of mind, evidence and social cooperation are changing.
No assertion of originality clearance is made until all 40 earlier texts have been inspected.

## Evidence anchors verified
- UNESCO (5 November 2025), adoption of first global normative framework for neurotechnology ethics: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ethics-neurotechnology-unesco-adopts-first-global-standard-cutting-edge-technology
- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology: https://www.unesco.org/en/node/86248
- OECD, 2026 Trust Survey executive summary (2025 data): https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-survey-on-drivers-of-trust-in-public-institutions-2026-results_9eb63fec-en/full-report/executive-summary_4319a992.html
- OECD 2026: 40% high/moderately high trust in national government versus 43% low/no trust across OECD participating countries. These are not global rates, nor measures of interpersonal trust. Only 31% believe people like them have a say in government decisions; do not infer causality from cross-sectional associations.

## Proposed topic refinement and non-overlap questions
01 当思想成为可以读取的数据 / When Thoughts Become Data
New lens: what can neural signals genuinely reveal, and how should inferred mental states be protected? No exaggerated claims of universal thought-reading. Contrast with Season IV's broad privacy focus.
02 意识之谜：我们究竟知道多少？ / Consciousness: What Do We Really Know?
New lens: experimental approaches, competing explanations, limits of inference. Do not present any single theory as settled.
03 当证据也需要身份证明 / When Evidence Needs a Provenance
New lens: chain of custody, authenticity, verification institutions, rather than generic deepfake alarmism. Needs independent sources.
04 信任从何处破裂，又如何修复？ / How Trust Breaks—and How It Can Be Rebuilt
New lens: institutional responsiveness, voice, accountability; distinguish government trust from interpersonal trust. OECD source supports institution-focused claims.
05 记忆可以改变，但我们还是自己吗？ / If Memory Changes, Are We Still Ourselves?
New lens: limits of reconsolidation and autobiographical identity; no fictional precision memory rewriting treated as established.
06 当机器成为倾诉对象 / When Machines Become Confidants
New lens: transparency, attachment, asymmetry, human connection. Do not claim machine sentience or therapeutic equivalence.
07 看似坚固的文明为何会失灵？ / Why Do Resilient Societies Fail?
New lens: cascading failures, infrastructure dependencies, response capacity; avoid sweeping deterministic collapse narratives.
08 人生意义能被科学测量吗？ / Can Science Measure a Meaningful Life?
New lens: science of wellbeing versus moral philosophy and plural values; avoid reducing meaning to a single metric.
09 当修复自然也可能带来新风险 / When Restoring Nature Creates New Risks
New lens: ecological interventions, uncertainty, governance and unintended consequences; distinct from Season IV's space stewardship. Evidence pending.
10 当文明学会重新审视自己 / When Civilization Learns to Question Itself
Finale: epistemic humility, institutional self-correction, intergenerational responsibility. No prophecy or grand awakening claim.
