## Cross-season editorial boundary
Published SIV05 《健康、长寿与生命的边界》 discusses personal health, privacy and consent; proposed V06 is **community-scale epidemiological measurement and operational interpretation**, not individual diagnosis or longevity.
Proposed SV02 《当我们误把相关当成原因》 should handle causal inference in general. V06 can explain the distinction between viral RNA concentration and case counts without a broad statistics lecture.
Proposed SV03 《当证据也需要身份证明》 focuses evidence provenance. V06 should avoid duplicating generic source-chain ethics and instead explain sampling chain, laboratory controls and reporting lags.
Proposed SV08 《当科学不能给出确定答案》 is broad uncertainty. V06 should show concrete, limited uncertainties: catchment representation, flow, assay and shedding.
Proposed SV09 《当修复自然也可能带来新风险》 is ecological restoration. V06 is public health, not wastewater remediation or environmental restoration.
