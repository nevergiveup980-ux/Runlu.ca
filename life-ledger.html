FROM RECORDS TO A WORKING TOOL

The spreadsheet was not discarded. It became the blueprint.

Built from real habits

Years of monthly records revealed the categories, merchants, weekly shopping rhythm, fuel frequency, and annual comparisons that actually matter in daily life.

One dataset, two views

Quick Add is designed for daily entry. Sheet View keeps the traditional month-by-month layout. Every new record appears in both automatically.

WHAT IT DOES

A few actions, kept deliberately clear.

01

Fast entry

Income, expense, and transfer records with amount, remembered item, category, account, date, and note.

02

Memory & suggestions

Items entered once become searchable suggestions; frequently used choices rise naturally to the top.

03

Sheet View

Items run down the left, months across the top, with separate weekly-style rows for recurring shopping and fuel entries.

04

Long-term statistics

Monthly, annual, category, merchant, income, expense, debt-payment, and net cash-flow totals remain continuously available.

05

Editable item library

Add, rename, recategorize, archive, or remove unused custom items without breaking historical records.

06

Portable records

The combined history can be exported as CSV, so the ledger never becomes a lock-in.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

No bank connection. No public ledger.

New records keep a local copy and can be synchronized through an encrypted backup. Encryption happens on the user’s device; the recovery key is never uploaded. The working app remains private and this public page contains no personal financial data.
