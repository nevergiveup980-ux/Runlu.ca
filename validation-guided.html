Research condition B · Task-first shortlist

Choose a workflow for meeting-note summaries

Fictional research prototype. These are hypothetical solution profiles, not actual vendors or verified product claims. No uploads, accounts, pricing, tracking or purchase links.

Your task

You have a fictional two-page meeting note. Choose the type of AI tool you would consider for creating a clear action-item summary. What would you verify before using it?

Three hypothetical approaches

Option A

General writing assistant

Useful when you can paste non-confidential text and specify a clear output format.

Trade-off: May omit or invent action owners; verify against the original.

Privacy: Check retention and training settings before sharing content.

Cost: Plan and usage limits unknown.

Option B

Document-focused assistant

Useful when the workflow depends on reading and referencing document sections.

Trade-off: File compatibility and citation accuracy need testing.

Privacy: Check upload, storage, deletion and access policies.

Cost: File limits and subscription terms unknown.

Option C

Local/offline workflow

Useful when avoiding cloud uploads is the first priority and local capability exists.

Trade-off: Setup, device requirements and summary quality vary.

Privacy: Confirm processing really remains on device.

Cost: Software and hardware costs unknown.

Before deciding

  • Confirm the tool's current capabilities and accuracy with fictional data.
  • Read current privacy, retention and deletion terms.
  • Verify total cost, usage limits and cancellation rules.
  • Review every extracted task, deadline and owner against the source.

Tell the moderator which approach you would try, why, and what evidence is missing. No choice is stored automatically.
