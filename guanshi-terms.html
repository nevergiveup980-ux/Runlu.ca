Service & Purchase Terms

These terms apply to paid RUNLU GUANSHI access purchased through an authorized RUNLU checkout. They supplement RUNLU's Privacy and Support information and any mandatory rights that apply to you.

What GUANSHI is

GUANSHI is a decision-support and reflection tool. It may combine modern evidence-oriented analysis with clearly identified traditional or symbolic lenses. It does not promise prediction accuracy, luck, guaranteed outcomes, or professional advice. For medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes matters, use appropriate qualified professional guidance and reliable evidence.

Plus subscriptions

Plus Monthly is CA$6.99 per month and Plus Annual is CA$59.99 per year, before any applicable taxes or checkout adjustments shown before purchase. A Plus subscription is recurring and renews automatically for the same billing interval until cancelled. The checkout must show the price and recurring interval before payment.

You may cancel future renewal through the subscription-management method provided by the payment provider or by contacting RUNLU Support. Cancellation stops future renewal; access normally continues through the paid billing period unless the payment provider, applicable law, or a refund decision requires otherwise.

Deep Reading

Deep Reading is a one-time CA$3.99 purchase for one deeper GUANSHI analysis credit. It is separate from Plus and does not create a recurring subscription. A credit is consumed when the corresponding Deep Reading is generated or otherwise fulfilled for the account.

Availability and fair use

Cloud features are subject to account limits, anti-abuse controls, service capacity, maintenance, and protected budget guardrails. Payment buys the stated access or capacity; it does not buy stronger claims, greater certainty, or a more favourable answer.

Refunds and payment provider

Refund requests are reviewed under applicable law and the rules of the payment provider. For purchases processed through Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe may handle support, subscription changes, disputes and eligible refunds under its own terms, including issuing refunds in circumstances permitted by its policies. Nothing here limits non-waivable consumer rights.

Support

For product-specific help, contact support@runlu.ca. Do not send payment-card details.
