RUNLU GUANSHI · V0.9 · T06

Bazi · The Grammar of Person × Time

Birth fact first · chart mechanics separated · claims tested

Four Pillars turns a birth moment into four stem–branch pairs: year, month, day and hour.

GUANSHI treats that conversion as one layer. Day Master, Ten Gods, strength, structures, useful gods and fate judgments belong to later interpretive layers and do not inherit factual status from the calendar.

四柱把一个出生时刻转换成年、月、日、时四组干支，共八个字。

观势把“排盘”只当作其中一层；日主、十神、旺衰、格局、用神以及人生吉凶判断属于后续传统解释层，不能因为历法换算正确就自动获得事实地位。

Les Quatre Piliers transforment un moment de naissance en quatre paires tronc–branche : année, mois, jour et heure.

GUANSHI sépare cette conversion des couches interprétatives ultérieures — maître du jour, Dix Dieux, force, structures, éléments utiles et jugements de destin.

Los Cuatro Pilares convierten un momento de nacimiento en cuatro pares tronco–rama: año, mes, día y hora.

GUANSHI separa esa conversión de las capas interpretativas posteriores — Maestro del Día, Diez Dioses, fuerza, estructuras, elementos útiles y juicios de destino.

A correct chart is not the same thing as a correct life prediction.

02

Five ledgers

Do not let one valid layer certify the next one.

1 · Birth facts

Date, clock time, place, time zone and calendar convention.

2 · Chart conversion

Convert the birth moment into four stem–branch pairs.

3 · Traditional model

Day Master, Ten Gods, strength, structures and luck cycles belong here.

4 · Life claims

Claims about personality, relationships, career, wealth, health or events require evidence.

5 · Outcome audit

Freeze predictions, compare with baselines, score outcomes and reweight.
