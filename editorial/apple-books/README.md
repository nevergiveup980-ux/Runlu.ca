# The Wind Beyond the Walls — Apple Books International Edition

Status: editorial working copy. These files are not wired into the live RUNLU website.

## Editorial principle

The Chinese reviewed manuscript remains the source text. English, French and Spanish editions should read as literary prose in their own language rather than as literal translations.

## International-edition rules

1. Preserve Chinese names, places, institutions and historical setting. Do not Westernize characters or locations.
2. At the first appearance of an unfamiliar Chinese place, add only enough context for a reader to orient themselves. Avoid footnote-like exposition in the narrative.
3. Explain culturally specific institutions through natural context. Terms such as the work-unit / danwei system should become understandable through the sentence and surrounding scene rather than through textbook exposition.
4. Preserve the quiet, restrained voice of the Chinese original. Do not add melodrama, moralizing or explanatory conclusions that are absent from the source.
5. Prefer idiomatic literary English, French and Spanish over sentence-by-sentence structural fidelity.
6. Keep paragraph rhythm and scene pacing close to the Chinese original where possible.
7. Names follow Chinese order: Zhang Yuansheng, with family name Zhang first. Do not reverse the name to Yuansheng Zhang in the narrative.
8. Romanization uses standard Hanyu Pinyin without tone marks unless a future publication-wide style decision changes this.
9. Avoid excessive glosses. A reader should feel guided, not lectured.
10. Publication label: RUNLU Original / International Edition.

## Chapter workflow

- Compare against the reviewed Chinese source.
- Revise English first to establish the cultural-context threshold.
- Edit French directly from the Chinese meaning and existing French draft, not by translating the revised English mechanically.
- Edit Spanish the same way.
- Run a final names, dates, geography and terminology consistency pass before EPUB production.
