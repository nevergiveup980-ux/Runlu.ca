# THE UNIVERSE WITHIN US — English Edition Master Plan

**Author:** JOHN ZHANG  
**Imprint:** RUNLU  
**Working subtitle:** The Human Body, Nature, and the Art of Living in Balance  
**Status:** English adaptation in development; not approved for publication.

## Editorial approach

Create a fluent, culturally legible English-language nonfiction book rather than a sentence-by-sentence translation. Preserve the Chinese edition's central proposition: the body is an open, adaptive system shaped by light, time, climate, relationships, labor, and environment. Explain *tian ren xiang ying* as a historical framework rather than presenting it as modern clinical proof. Distinguish metaphor, historical interpretation, experimental findings, and practical recommendations.

Adapt ordinary-life vignettes for an English-language readership where natural, but do not invent research or imply the historical Chinese sources originated in the West. Keep the author's calm, humane, evidence-aware voice.

## Production gates

1. Translate/adapt in coherent multi-chapter sections against the locked Chinese proof; do not shorten scientific qualifications.
2. Verify each English factual claim and map citations to the original 18-entry bibliography, correcting citation titles and links where needed.
3. Review English prose for idiomatic language, consistency, pacing, and avoidance of unsupported medical claims.
4. Assemble the English proof, then build an EPUB 3 with English language metadata, navigable contents, working references, and an English-language cover.
5. Run EPUBCheck and perform actual device reading tests before publication. ISBN, copyright permissions, and final publishing metadata remain pending.
