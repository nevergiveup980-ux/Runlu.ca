## A Note to Readers

This book explores historical ideas, contemporary health science, and the conditions of everyday living. It is intended for general education and reflection. It does not provide an individual diagnosis, treatment plan, or prescription.

Illustrative scenes and composite examples are fictional unless expressly identified otherwise. They should not be interpreted as case reports or evidence that an intervention is effective.

For persistent or severe symptoms, questions about medications or supplements, or health decisions involving particular medical conditions, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. Seek local emergency services when urgent medical attention is needed. Do not discontinue prescribed treatment on the basis of this book.

Scientific understanding changes. Sources cited here reflect selected evidence and guidance; readers should consult updated recommendations relevant to their circumstances.
