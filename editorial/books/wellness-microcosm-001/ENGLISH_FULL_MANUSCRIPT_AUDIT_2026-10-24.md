## Findings

1. All thirteen planned narrative sections exist in the branch.
2. All numbered reference IDs 1–18 appear at least once in the English manuscript, and no out-of-range numbered reference IDs were detected in this scan.
3. Citation-number coverage does not establish that each citation supports its nearby claim. Verify source text, publication details, working URLs, and strength of evidence before approval.
4. The shorter sections include the epilogue and Chapter Two, but length alone does not justify adding filler.
5. Chapters Seven through Eleven contain many subsection headings; evaluate whether the reading experience is too fragmented compared with Chapters One through Six.
6. Several chapter files still identify themselves as working drafts. Standardize edition metadata only after the text is locked.
7. The English references and notices exist as a separate back-matter draft; they have not yet been integrated into a validated EPUB.

The English manuscript has complete structural coverage and consistent citation numbering. It is **not yet publication-ready**; source validation, copy-editing, and EPUB testing remain open.
