# RUNLU WELLNESS 002 — Editorial production brief

**Working Chinese title:** 人是一个小宇宙：从天人相应到生命的整体和谐
**English working title:** The Human Body as a Microcosm: Life in Rhythm with Nature
**Status:** Editorial blueprint, not a completed manuscript or publication.
**Author credit:** JOHN ZHANG · RUNLU

## Thesis
Traditional Chinese medicine's 天人相应 offers a historically grounded framework for asking how humans relate to time, climate, place, emotion, community, and nature. It is a philosophical and medical tradition, not proof of literal astronomical-organ correspondence. Contemporary evidence should be used to test particular health claims, without treating either tradition or science as a prop for the other.

## Long-form architecture (Chinese master, approximately 8,000–12,000 Hanzi)
1. 序：仰观天地，俯察生命 — microcosm as a metaphor, not a literal cosmology.
2. 天人相应的思想源流 — contextualize 《黄帝内经》, distinguish classical texts and later interpretations.
3. 天时：日月、四时与生物节律 — circadian timing, light, sleep, seasonal variability.
4. 地利：气候、地理与环境适应 — heat, cold, altitude, humidity, air quality.
5. 人体：稳态、反馈与适应 — physiology, interconnected systems, no facile yin-yang equivalence.
6. 情志：精神与身体的相互影响 — stress, emotion, social relationships; avoid overclaiming.
7. 人和：劳动、家庭与社会 — work schedules, support, socioeconomic and cultural conditions.
8. 东西方知识体系如何对话 — evidence hierarchy, what can be tested, what remains metaphor.
9. 因时、因地、因人而养 — practical habits, individualized limits, no diagnosis or cure promises.
10. 终章：天地有序，生命有度 — nuanced, actionable synthesis.

## Evidence and safety
- Cite primary classical sources with exact chapter/quotation and translations reviewed.
- Ground biomedical claims in trustworthy reviews, consensus guidelines, or primary studies; link citations near claims.
- Clearly label metaphor, traditional theory, observational evidence, and intervention evidence.
- Avoid implying planetary motion controls specific organs or that all traditional hour-based regimens are clinically proven.
- Avoid prescriptive claims for pregnancy, chronic disease, medications, or individual treatment.
- Include educational-not-medical-advice notice and accessibility-minded alt text.
