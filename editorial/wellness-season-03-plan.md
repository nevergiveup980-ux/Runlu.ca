### Editorial boundaries
- Evidence-informed, useful, literary but plainspoken; no claims of reversing age or curing disease.
- Clearly distinguish physical activity, structured exercise, sitting, sleep, and recovery.
- WHO/Canadian guidance: most adults target 150–300 minutes/week of moderate aerobic activity, plus major-muscle strengthening on at least two days; older adults also benefit from balance training. These are population guidelines, not universal personal prescriptions.
- Any activity is better than none; begin within individual ability and progress gradually.
- If symptoms, limitations, chronic conditions, pregnancy, injury or fall risks exist, adapt appropriately and seek qualified advice.
- The four versions ZH/EN/FR/ES should be substantive, readable, correctly linked, and reviewed separately.
- Release one lecture at a time following source QA; update wellness.html only when the four article versions are ready for publication.
- Never call a lecture published until its PR is merged. Live mobile acceptance belongs to the user.
