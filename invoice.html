RUNLU STUDIO · iOS

Universal
Invoice

A native iPhone invoicing workspace for flexible customer schedules, clean document generation, and human-controlled email sending.

App Store CandidateiOS V3.0 · RC4

Source-audited and being finalized through Xcode, real-device, StoreKit sandbox, and TestFlight validation before submission.

WHAT IT INCLUDES

Built around real billing routines.

01

Dynamic customers

Start with zero customers and add the profiles your work needs — the runtime is not fixed to two customers.

02

Flexible billing schedules

One-time, weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, or custom-day schedules, with a preferred send time per customer.

03

Word invoice generation

Generate a reusable Word invoice from the customer and business details stored in the workspace.

04

Email + attachment, prepared

On iPhone with Apple Mail configured, one action prepares recipient, subject, message, and the generated invoice attachment. You still tap Send yourself.

05

StoreKit 2 Professional access

Auto-renewable monthly access with purchase restoration and Apple-managed billing.

06

Offline-first workspace

Core business and customer settings are designed to remain on the device in this release, without advertising or tracking SDKs.
