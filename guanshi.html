RUNLU GUANSHI

观势·Trend Dynamics

Open framework · V0.1

Observe the forces. Understand the change. Decide with evidence. Review through practice.

GUANSHI is not an attempt to create a more mysterious form of fortune-telling. It is an evolving decision framework: traditional Chinese ways of observing change are treated as cultural and heuristic models, modern decision science supplies structure, and real outcomes are used to correct the system over time.

观其势，察其变，据其实，而后决；再以实践反证与修正。

观势并不是为了制造一种更神秘的“算命术”。它是一套持续演化的决策体系：把中国传统关于变化、时间、环境与人的观察方式作为文化与启发式模型，以现代科学决策方法搭建结构，再用真实结果不断检验、修正和淘汰。

Observer les forces. Comprendre le changement. Décider avec des éléments probants. Réviser par la pratique.

GUANSHI ne cherche pas à inventer une forme plus mystérieuse de divination. C’est un cadre décisionnel évolutif : les traditions chinoises d’observation du changement sont traitées comme modèles culturels et heuristiques, les méthodes modernes de décision apportent la structure, et les résultats réels servent à corriger le système dans le temps.

Observar las fuerzas. Comprender el cambio. Decidir con evidencia. Revisar mediante la práctica.

GUANSHI no pretende crear una forma más misteriosa de adivinación. Es un marco de decisión en evolución: las formas tradicionales chinas de observar el cambio se tratan como modelos culturales y heurísticos, la ciencia moderna de la decisión aporta estructura y los resultados reales se utilizan para corregir el sistema con el tiempo.

If a traditional claim conflicts with reliable evidence, evidence wins.

01

The foundation

Four layers. One feedback loop.

01

Science

Probability, decision theory, Bayesian updating, behavioral science, systems thinking and risk management form the technical foundation.

02

Reality

Current conditions, resources, constraints, history, environment and observable behavior are treated as primary inputs.

03

Tradition

Yijing, yin-yang, five phases, bagua, stems and branches, feng shui and related traditions are preserved as interpretive models—not automatic facts.

04

Validation

Every useful judgment should be recordable, reviewable and revisable. Models that repeatedly fail lose weight; models that add value earn further testing.

02

Nine lenses

From change to context to action.

易

Change

What is moving, emerging, declining or turning?

象

Pattern

What recurring structure or relationship can be observed?

数

Measure

What can be quantified, compared, estimated or assigned uncertainty?

时

Time

Is this a moment to initiate, wait, consolidate or exit?

地

Environment

How do place, space, light, movement, access and external conditions shape the choice?

人

Person

What are the person’s goals, abilities, habits, biases, resources and limits?

事

Situation

What exactly is being decided, and which facts are decision-relevant?

变

Scenarios

What plausible paths could unfold, and what would change the probabilities?

势

Dynamics

What is the combined direction, strength, uncertainty and action advantage now?

03

Decision loop

A judgment is only useful if it can be checked later.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Separate facts, assumptions, emotions and traditional interpretations.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Define the decision, objective, constraints, alternatives and time horizon.

  3. 03

    Estimate

    Use evidence, base rates, scenarios and explicit uncertainty instead of certainty language.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Choose an action that balances expected value, downside risk, reversibility and timing.

  5. 05

    Record

    Save the inputs, model contributions, confidence level and reasons before the result is known.

  6. 06

    Review

    Compare forecast with outcome; update weights, assumptions and future decisions.

04

How results should sound

From destiny language to decision language.

Avoid

“You are destined to leave this job.”

Prefer

“Current evidence modestly favors preparing to move rather than resigning immediately. The largest uncertainty is income stability; reassess when that changes.”

05

Boundaries

Curious about tradition. Strict about claims.

GUANSHI may study Yijing, bagua, feng shui, divination traditions, birth-chart systems and other historical practices, but their inclusion does not establish scientific validity. Each concept must be labeled by evidence status and separated from verified facts.

High-stakes medical, legal, financial, safety or mental-health decisions should never be based on divination or traditional symbolism. GUANSHI can help organize questions and uncertainty, but it does not replace qualified professional advice.

观势可以研究《易经》、八卦、风水、占卜、命理及其他历史传统，但“纳入研究”并不等于“已经被科学证实”。每个概念都应标注证据状态，并与已验证事实清楚分开。

医疗、法律、投资、安全、心理健康等高风险决策，绝不能依据占卜或传统象征来决定。观势可以帮助梳理问题与不确定性，但不能替代合格专业人士的意见。

GUANSHI peut étudier Yijing, bagua, feng shui, traditions divinatoires, systèmes astrologiques de naissance et autres pratiques historiques, mais leur présence ne prouve pas leur validité scientifique. Chaque concept doit indiquer son statut de preuve et rester distinct des faits vérifiés.

Les décisions à fort enjeu — médicales, juridiques, financières, de sécurité ou de santé mentale — ne doivent jamais reposer sur la divination ou le symbolisme traditionnel. GUANSHI peut aider à structurer questions et incertitudes, sans remplacer un professionnel qualifié.

GUANSHI puede estudiar Yijing, bagua, feng shui, tradiciones adivinatorias, sistemas de carta natal y otras prácticas históricas, pero incluirlas no demuestra validez científica. Cada concepto debe indicar su estado de evidencia y mantenerse separado de los hechos verificados.

Las decisiones médicas, legales, financieras, de seguridad o salud mental de alto impacto nunca deben basarse en adivinación o simbolismo tradicional. GUANSHI puede ayudar a ordenar preguntas e incertidumbre, pero no sustituye el consejo de profesionales cualificados.
