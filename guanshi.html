Observe the forces. Understand the change. Decide with evidence. Review through practice.

GUANSHI is not an attempt to create a more mysterious form of fortune-telling. It is an evolving decision framework: traditional ways of observing change are preserved as cultural and heuristic lenses, modern decision science supplies structure, and real outcomes correct the system over time.

观其势，察其变，据其实，而后决；再以实践反证与修正。

观势并不是为了制造一种更神秘的“算命术”。它是一套持续演化的决策体系：传统关于变化、时间、环境与人的观察方式作为文化与启发式视角保留，以现代科学决策方法搭建结构，再用真实结果不断检验、修正和淘汰。

Observer les forces. Comprendre le changement. Décider avec des éléments probants. Réviser par la pratique.

GUANSHI ne cherche pas à inventer une divination plus mystérieuse. C’est un cadre décisionnel évolutif : les façons traditionnelles d’observer le changement sont conservées comme angles culturels et heuristiques, la science moderne de la décision apporte la structure, et les résultats réels corrigent le système avec le temps.

Observar las fuerzas. Comprender el cambio. Decidir con evidencia. Revisar mediante la práctica.

GUANSHI no busca inventar una adivinación más misteriosa. Es un marco de decisión en evolución: las formas tradicionales de observar el cambio se conservan como perspectivas culturales y heurísticas, la ciencia moderna de decisiones aporta estructura y los resultados reales corrigen el sistema con el tiempo.

If a traditional claim conflicts with reliable evidence, evidence wins.

01

The foundation

Four layers. One feedback loop.

01

Science

Probability, decision theory, Bayesian updating, behavioral science, systems thinking, scenario analysis and risk management form the technical foundation.

02

Reality

Current conditions, resources, constraints, history, environment and observable behavior are primary inputs.

03

Tradition

Yijing, yin-yang, five phases, bagua, stems and branches, Bazi, divination and feng shui are preserved as interpretive models—not automatic facts.

04

Validation

Judgments are recorded before outcomes, then reviewed. Repeated failures lose weight; useful signals earn further testing.

02

Nine lenses

From change to context to action.

易

Change

What is emerging, declining, turning or transforming?

象

Pattern

What recurring structures or relationships can be observed?

数

Measure

What can be quantified, compared or assigned uncertainty?

时

Time

What horizon and timing matter to the choice?

地

Environment

How do place, layout, access and external conditions shape the choice?

人

Person

What goals, abilities, habits, biases, resources and limits matter?

事

Situation

What concrete decision is actually being made?

变

Scenarios

How might the situation evolve under plausible futures?

势

Dynamics

After integrating evidence, uncertainty and context, which direction has the strongest momentum?

03

Decision loop

No prediction without a future review.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Separate known facts from assumptions and unknowns.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Define the real decision, alternatives, time horizon and stakes.

  3. 03

    Estimate

    Use base rates, probabilities, scenarios and explicit uncertainty where possible.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Choose the most robust action, not the most dramatic story.

  5. 05

    Record

    Freeze the judgment before the outcome is known, including confidence and falsification conditions.

  6. 06

    Review & reweight

    Compare prediction with outcome. Reward added value; reduce weight for repeated failure or post-hoc explanation.

04

The difference

From fate claims to decision support.

Not GUANSHI

“You will definitely succeed.”

GUANSHI

“Given current facts, base rates and constraints, option B appears more robust.”

05

Boundaries

Curiosity without surrendering judgment.

Traditional symbolism must never override reliable facts.

For high-stakes decisions, traditional lenses may provide cultural context only.
