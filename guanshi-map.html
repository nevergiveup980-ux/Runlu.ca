Knowledge Map

Two knowledge lines · one evidence discipline

Tradition may suggest a lens. Science determines what may be claimed. Practice decides what survives.

The map does not place every idea on the same evidential level. It preserves cultural models, separates empirical claims from symbolism, and connects both to a modern decision loop.

传统可以提供观察视角；科学决定什么可以被声称；实践决定什么最终留下。

这张地图不会把所有知识放在同一个证据等级上。我们保留文化模型，区分经验事实、可检验假设与象征解释，并把它们接入现代决策闭环。

La tradition peut proposer un angle d’observation. La science détermine ce qui peut être affirmé. La pratique décide de ce qui reste.

La carte ne place pas toutes les idées au même niveau de preuve. Elle conserve les modèles culturels, sépare les affirmations empiriques du symbolisme et relie l’ensemble à une boucle décisionnelle moderne.

La tradición puede aportar una perspectiva. La ciencia determina qué puede afirmarse. La práctica decide qué permanece.

El mapa no coloca todas las ideas en el mismo nivel de evidencia. Conserva modelos culturales, separa afirmaciones empíricas de interpretaciones simbólicas y conecta todo con un ciclo moderno de decisión.

Traditional knowledge line

Preserve the lens. Test the claim.

T01

Yijing · Change

Change, opposition, transition, timing and the relation between stable structure and changing conditions.

Symbolic / philosophical
T02

Yin–Yang

A relational vocabulary for contrast, balance, alternation and context—not a physical force unless separately demonstrated.

Symbolic model
T03

Five Phases

A historical system of cycles and relationships. Its metaphors can organize observation; causal claims require independent evidence.

Traditional
T04

Bagua · Pattern

Eight archetypal pattern categories used to compare states and transitions, retained as a classification lens rather than a proven predictive engine.

Traditional
T05

Stems, Branches & Calendrical Cycles

Retained as historical time-encoding systems. Calendar facts are factual; personality or destiny claims are treated separately and require testing.

Mixed evidence types
T06

Bazi / Destiny Models

Preserved as historical pattern systems. Deterministic claims are not assumed true; testable predictions must be preregistered and reviewed against outcomes.

Traditional / test required
T07

Divination Methods

May be studied as structured prompts for reframing a question, but must not bypass evidence, probability or professional judgment in high-stakes decisions.

Exploratory
T08

Feng Shui · Environment

Separated into measurable environmental factors—light, noise, airflow, access, layout, movement—and traditional symbolic rules, which are labeled independently.

Separate measurable from symbolic

02

Modern decision line

Estimate uncertainty before choosing.

M01

Probability

Replace absolute predictions with ranges, base rates and explicit uncertainty.

Core method
M02

Bayesian Updating

Start with prior plausibility, add new evidence, and revise confidence rather than protecting the first belief.

Core method
M03

Decision Trees

Make alternatives, consequences, probabilities and trade-offs visible before action.

Core method
M04

Behavioral Science

Check confirmation bias, loss aversion, framing, overconfidence, sunk-cost effects and other predictable distortions.

Core method
M05

Risk & Robustness

Prefer choices that remain acceptable when assumptions are wrong, especially when downside is large or irreversible.

Core method
M06

Systems Thinking

Look for feedback loops, delays, bottlenecks, second-order effects and interactions instead of isolating one variable.

Core method
M07

Scenario Analysis

Compare plausible futures instead of pretending one future can be known with certainty.

Core method
M08

Decision Journal & Calibration

Record forecasts before outcomes are known, then measure accuracy, confidence calibration and recurring errors.

Validation engine

Convergence

Everything converges on 势.

势Integrated direction

GUANSHI does not ask, “What is my fixed fate?”

It asks: What is happening now? What is changing? Which forces matter? What can be measured? What remains uncertain? Which action is robust? What happened after we acted?

观势不问：“我的命是不是已经注定？”

它问的是：现在发生了什么？什么正在变化？哪些力量真正重要？什么可以测量？什么仍然不确定？哪种行动更稳健？行动之后实际发生了什么？

GUANSHI ne demande pas : « Mon destin est-il fixé ? »

Il demande : que se passe-t-il maintenant ? Qu’est-ce qui change ? Quelles forces comptent ? Que peut-on mesurer ? Que reste-t-il d’incertain ? Quelle action est robuste ? Qu’est-il réellement arrivé après l’action ?

GUANSHI no pregunta: «¿Mi destino ya está fijado?»

Pregunta: ¿qué está ocurriendo ahora? ¿Qué cambia? ¿Qué fuerzas importan? ¿Qué puede medirse? ¿Qué sigue siendo incierto? ¿Qué acción es robusta? ¿Qué ocurrió realmente después de actuar?
