Object 05Archive piece

RUNLU Dragon & Phoenix Cap

An earlier RUNLU object, kept in the story rather than redesigned away.

A deep-navy cap from an earlier RUNLU visual period, embroidered in gold with a dragon-and-phoenix composition, a green center and the line “RISE FROM WITHIN.” Its style differs from today’s quieter Goods language, which is exactly why it belongs in the archive.

State
Archive piece
Palette
Deep navy · Gold · Emerald green
Role
Early RUNLU record

Archive does not mean obsolete. It means the object is kept with its original character and context intact.

Future objects.

These are concept studies, not finished products. We are giving them a place in the archive now so they can grow slowly, be tested later, and become real only when the material and purpose feel right.
