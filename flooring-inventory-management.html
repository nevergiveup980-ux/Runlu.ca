Flooring Inventory Management

One inventory environment for roll goods, carton goods and flooring supplies.

Flooring-specific workflowBuilt around real material movement

Flooring inventory mixes materials that behave differently. Carpet is measured and cut by length; plank and tile often move by carton; underlayment and adhesives have their own units. RUNLU keeps these different material types inside one connected warehouse workflow.
