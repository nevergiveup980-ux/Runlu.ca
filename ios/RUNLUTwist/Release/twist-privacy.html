Privacy / 隐私

RUNLU TWIST 1.0 · 2026-10-24

The app works offline. Progress, hints, self-assessment and language preference are stored only on your device. RUNLU TWIST has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not send gameplay data to RUNLU.

Sharing sends only the result text you choose to a destination you select in the iOS share sheet. That destination’s privacy policy applies. Reset progress clears puzzle progress; language preference remains. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

应用离线运行。进度、提示、自评和语言偏好仅保存在本机，无账户、广告、分析或追踪 SDK，不向润庐发送游戏数据。分享只向你选择的目标发送成绩文字。重置清除答题进度，保留语言偏好；卸载移除本地数据。
