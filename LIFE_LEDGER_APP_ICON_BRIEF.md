# RUNLU Ledger · App Icon Brief

## Goal
Create a serious, modern personal-finance utility icon that communicates **two working views over one ledger** without looking like a bank, crypto wallet, spreadsheet clone, or accounting package.

## Lead visual
- Square App Store icon composition with Apple-style rounded-corner safe framing.
- Deep RUNLU navy background.
- One restrained RUNLU green accent.
- Central geometric monogram that can read as both `RL` and two offset ledger panels.
- The two panels should share one central axis or baseline to imply one dataset.
- Strong silhouette at small size.

## Avoid
- dollar signs;
- coins;
- bank buildings;
- crypto motifs;
- tiny charts;
- full word RUNLU;
- multiple colors;
- glossy skeuomorphic money imagery;
- literal spreadsheet cells that become illegible at 60px.

## Suggested geometry
A compact mark formed by two vertical rounded rectangles/panels:
- rear panel: navy-to-charcoal relief;
- front panel: restrained green edge or inset;
- a negative-space cut creates the suggestion of `R` on the left and `L` on the right;
- slight offset between panels = Entries / Sheet View;
- one shared base = one set of data.

## Rendering direction
Minimal, premium utility, flat-to-subtle-depth, crisp edges, no text. It should remain legible against both light and dark App Store backgrounds.

## Acceptance test
At thumbnail size, the icon should still read as one strong symbol. At full size, the two-view idea should become visible without explanation.
