# RUNLU Ledger · App Icon Brief

Status: **LOCKED launch direction** for App Store staging.

## Source of truth
Use the existing RUNLU unified brand mark supplied by the owner:
- translucent blue/cyan oval water-drop badge;
- dark outline;
- black RUNLU wordmark;
- small droplet mark above RUNLU;
- blue wave below;
- bright, clean, glass-like highlight treatment.

This existing oval droplet identity is the brand asset. Do **not** replace it with a new RL monogram, geometric ledger symbol, coin, chart, bank, or crypto-style icon.

## App icon treatment
The App Store icon may adapt the supplied mark only to improve small-size legibility:
- place the oval RUNLU badge prominently inside a square app-icon canvas;
- use deep RUNLU navy/blue as the surrounding field;
- keep the oval large enough that RUNLU remains recognizable at Home Screen size;
- preserve the droplet, RUNLU wordmark, oval rim, and wave as the defining family identity;
- keep highlights crisp but avoid visual noise around the outer corners;
- ensure no alpha/transparency remains in the final exported App Store icon asset.

## Product-specific differentiation
RUNLU Ledger should **not** alter the master logo to explain the two-view feature. Product differentiation belongs in:
- the App Store name: `RUNLU Ledger`;
- subtitle;
- screenshots;
- in-app Home / Entries / Sheet View presentation.

The icon's job is RUNLU family recognition first.

## Avoid
- redesigning the master RUNLU mark;
- replacing the oval badge with a standalone water droplet;
- replacing it with an RL monogram;
- adding dollar signs, coins, bank buildings, crypto motifs, mini charts, or tiny spreadsheet grids;
- adding `Ledger` inside the icon;
- changing the established RUNLU lettering;
- adding a second competing logo language.

## Acceptance test
1. At full size, the icon clearly uses the existing RUNLU oval translucent-water identity.
2. At Home Screen size, the oval silhouette and RUNLU word remain recognizable.
3. It looks like a member of the same RUNLU product family before it looks like a generic finance app.
4. The final export is a valid square App Store icon asset with no transparent pixels.

## Current launch choice
Use the approved adaptation built from the existing RUNLU oval droplet badge on a deep blue square field as the working icon for App Store Candidate V2.3.
