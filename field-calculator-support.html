Version 1.0 support

Before you report a problem

  • Confirm the calculator module you were using: Carpet, Roll Length, Sheet Goods, Plank / Tile, Underlay, Adhesive, Floor Prep, Stairs, or Mortar / Grout.
  • Include the values you entered immediately before the issue occurred.
  • Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and RUNLU Field Calculator version.
  • Do not send customer-sensitive or confidential information unless it is necessary to diagnose the issue.

Common questions

Does the calculator work offline?
Core calculations are designed to work without an internet connection.

Where are saved materials and jobs stored?
Saved material settings, remnants and job items in version 1.0 are stored locally on the device.

Are calculated quantities final ordering instructions?
No. RUNLU Field Calculator is a planning and estimating tool. Always verify site conditions, product data, coverage rates and manufacturer installation requirements before final ordering.

Why does an adhesive, mortar or grout calculation ask for coverage?
Coverage can vary by product, trowel, substrate, tile size and application. RUNLU does not invent a universal coverage value; use the applicable manufacturer data.
