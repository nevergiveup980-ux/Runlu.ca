# RUNLU Commercial & Advertising Policy

Effective: 2026-08-30

## Core rule

RUNLU does not use conventional display advertising as a default website monetization model.

This includes banner ads, pop-ups, interstitial ads, auto-inserted ad networks, disruptive sponsored widgets, or ad placements that weaken the calm, minimal, editorial character of RUNLU.

## Preferred monetization

RUNLU should first earn through its own products, works, and services, including software, digital products, books, original works, merchandise, subscriptions, and other RUNLU-created offerings.

The principle is:

> RUNLU may earn revenue, but the site should not look as though revenue extraction is its purpose.

## Sponsorship exception

A limited sponsor or partner relationship may be considered only when all of the following are true:

1. The sponsor is genuinely relevant to RUNLU's subject matter, audience, values, or projects.
2. The placement is quiet, limited, and visually subordinate to the content.
3. Sponsorship is clearly identified as `Sponsor`, `Partner`, `Supported by`, or an equivalent disclosure.
4. Paid support never determines RUNLU's editorial conclusions, opinions, rankings, reviews, research results, or product recommendations.
5. Sponsored material is never disguised as independent editorial content.
6. User privacy, trust, and reading experience take priority over sponsor revenue.

## Default decision rule

When there is doubt about whether an advertisement or sponsorship belongs on RUNLU, the default answer is **do not place it**.

Any future move from this policy toward conventional advertising should be treated as a deliberate brand-level decision rather than a routine content or operations change.

# 润庐商业化与广告规则

生效日期：2026-08-30

## 核心规则

RUNLU 网站原则上不采用传统展示广告作为默认盈利方式。

包括但不限于：横幅广告、弹窗广告、插屏广告、自动广告网络、干扰阅读的赞助组件，以及任何破坏 RUNLU 安静、克制、极简气质的广告形式。

## 优先商业化方式

RUNLU 优先通过自己的产品、作品与服务获得收入，包括软件、数字产品、书籍、原创作品、周边商品、订阅以及其它 RUNLU 自有产品与服务。

核心原则：

> RUNLU 可以赚钱，但不要让人一眼看上去像是在赚钱。

## 赞助例外

未来可以考虑少量 Sponsor / Partner 合作，但必须同时满足：

1. 与 RUNLU 的内容、读者、理念或项目确实相关；
2. 展示克制、有限，不喧宾夺主；
3. 必须清楚注明 Sponsor、Partner、Supported by 或同等含义的标识；
4. 赞助方不得影响 RUNLU 的观点、结论、排名、评论、研究结果或产品推荐；
5. 赞助内容不得伪装成独立编辑内容；
6. 用户隐私、信任和阅读体验永远优先于赞助收入。

## 默认判断原则

当某个广告或赞助是否适合 RUNLU 存在疑问时，默认选择：**不上。**

未来如要改变这一规则、引入传统广告，应视为品牌级重大决定，而不是日常运营事项。
