PRIVACY POLICY

RUNLU Ledger

RUNLU Ledger is designed as a private personal income and expense ledger. The current version does not require a bank connection and does not collect personal or financial data from users.

Data collection

RUNLU Ledger does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data, financial data, usage data, or device data.

Local storage

Ledger records created in the app are stored locally on the user’s device. RUNLU does not operate a server that receives or stores those ledger records in the current version of the app.

Bank connections

RUNLU Ledger does not connect to banks, credit-card providers, payment accounts, or other financial institutions.

Advertising and tracking

RUNLU Ledger does not use advertising networks, third-party tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

The current version of RUNLU Ledger does not use third-party analytics or advertising services to collect data from the app.

Data export

If the app provides an export function, the user controls when and where exported records are saved or shared. RUNLU does not receive a copy of those exported records.

Children’s privacy

RUNLU Ledger is a general-purpose personal ledger and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled — for example by adding cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other network services — this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent through the RUNLU support page.
