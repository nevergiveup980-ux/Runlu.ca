What the product is

RUNLU Deerfoot Flooring OS is not a generic invoice app and not a collection of disconnected forms. It is a workflow system organized around one shared business record: the Order.

Enter business information once. Let every department see the part it needs. Keep normal work quiet and surface the exceptions that need human attention.

The system was shaped directly from real Deerfoot Flooring routines: the physical Active / Back Order drawer, supplier pickup folders, receiving work, People TO Call, installer scheduling, the three-copy Deerfoot invoice, payment closeout and accounting review.

Workflow patterns unique to this system

Orders as a digital filing drawer

Active Orders and Back Orders mirror the real physical drawer. Customer/company names beginning with numbers appear first, followed by natural A–Z order. Search can also use customer, phone, Order #, Invoice # or PO #.

Supplier Pickup stays separate

Supplier Pickup is an inbound procurement file system, not a subfolder of Orders. The Order only shows the linked procurement status. This preserves the way the real business works while eliminating duplicated data.

People TO Call is a work queue

It is not an inventory status. It tells Sales that material from a supplier pickup has been received and a human follow-up is required before the next customer-facing step.

Calendar is a view over business records

Calendar direct edit and source-form edit operate on the same records. Schedule span, work progress, material readiness and issues are shown as separate visual signals.

Accounting is a control layer

Instead of leading with debit/credit terminology, Accounting starts with Receivable, Payable, GST Estimate, Job Profit and Attention. PO ↔ Receiving ↔ Vendor Bill matching and the Financial Trail make it possible to trace where a number came from.

Current product boundary

The workflow is complete enough for end-to-end learning and field testing, but deployment maturity is a separate subject. Several records in the current build are still stored in browser/local storage or operational stores. Bank feeds and a formal General Ledger are not connected, and Accounting is a review/control layer rather than a tax-filing system.

Recommended production posture: continue validating real workflows, add central server-backed persistence/backup and permissions before treating the system as the sole statutory book of record. Tax, payroll, WCB, T5018 and construction-law decisions should remain subject to accountant/professional review.

Release philosophy

V0.3.63 remains the explicit stable rendering rollback baseline. Later workflow layers were added incrementally so each major improvement can be isolated, tested and rolled back without disturbing unrelated RUNLU projects.

This is the central RUNLU rule behind the product: small changes should be reversible; source-of-truth data should not be duplicated; and the software should follow the real work rather than forcing the real work to follow the software.

它是什么

RUNLU Deerfoot Flooring OS 不是一个普通开票软件，也不是很多彼此独立的表格拼在一起。它围绕同一份核心业务记录——Order——组织整个公司工作。

业务资料尽量只录一次；不同部门看到自己需要的部分；正常业务保持安静，真正需要人处理的异常主动浮出来。

系统直接取材于 Deerfoot Flooring 的真实工作习惯：Active / Back Order 实体抽屉、Supplier Pickup 文件夹、Receiving、People TO Call、installer calendar、三联 Deerfoot invoice、payment closeout，以及后续 Accounting review。

几套最有 RUNLU 特点的工作方式

Orders 就是数字文件抽屉

Active 与 Back 分列，复刻真实文件柜。数字开头的公司名排在最前，然后自然 A–Z；也可以按客户、电话、Order #、Invoice #、PO # 搜索。

Supplier Pickup 保持独立

去供应商仓库取货属于 inbound procurement，它不是 Orders 的子文件夹。Order 只显示关联状态，真正的 Pickup 文件仍在自己的模块里。

People TO Call 是工作队列

它不是库存状态。它表达的是：材料已经通过 Supplier Pickup 收到，但 Sales 还需要做一次人为跟进，才能进入 Customer Pickup、Delivery 或 Installation 等下一步。

Calendar 是业务数据的可视化编辑器

Calendar Direct Edit 与源表单编辑的是同一份记录，不另外造第二套排班数据库。时间跨度、工作进度、材料 readiness、冲突问题分别表达。

Accounting 是控制中心

Accounting 先回答 Receivable、Payable、GST Estimate、Job Profit、Attention，而不是先让普通员工学习 Debit/Credit。PO ↔ Receiving ↔ Vendor Bill 的 Match，加上 Financial Trail，让每个数字都能追到来源。

当前产品边界

目前的工作流已经足以进行完整的 end-to-end 学习和现场测试，但“业务功能完成”和“企业部署成熟”是两件事。当前部分记录仍使用 browser/local storage 或 operational store；Bank feed 与正式 General Ledger 尚未连接；Accounting 是 review/control layer，不是自动报税系统。

正式生产前建议：继续用真实业务验证流程；补齐中央数据库、备份和权限；在把系统作为唯一法定账簿之前完成 server-backed persistence。GST、Payroll、WCB、T5018、construction law 等专业判断仍应由 Accountant / 专业人士复核。

版本与开发原则

V0.3.63 继续作为明确的稳定渲染回滚基线。后续功能采用一层一层增量方式加入，方便每次测试、回退，也避免碰到 RUNLU 其它项目。

这套产品背后的长期原则是：小改动尽量可逆；source-of-truth 不重复；软件去适应真实工作，而不是逼真实工作去迁就软件。
