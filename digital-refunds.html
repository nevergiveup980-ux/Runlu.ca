Refund Policy

RUNLU DIGITAL products are downloadable digital goods. Because access can be provided immediately after purchase, refunds are generally not offered for change of mind after the product has been accessed or downloaded.

When we will review a refund request

Please contact us promptly if you were charged more than once for the same order, the delivered files are materially defective or different from the product description, or a technical delivery problem cannot be resolved. We will review reasonable requests individually.

How to request help

Email hello@runlu.ca with the email address used at checkout, the product name, and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment card details.

Payment-provider decisions and legal rights

If a merchant-of-record processes the purchase, it may issue refunds or handle chargebacks under its own rules. This policy does not override mandatory consumer rights or other rights that apply by law.
