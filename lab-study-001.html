STUDY 001
Long-term human–AI cognitive coupling.

研究 001
长期的人与 AI 认知耦合。

Notre terme de travail est le couplage cognitif : deux participants développent progressivement références communes, habitudes de correction et règles de décision. « Intrication » sert uniquement d’inspiration pour étudier une coordination corrélée, sans affirmation quantique sur l’esprit ou le logiciel.

01 · RESEARCH QUESTION

Does continuity create a measurable coordination advantage?

Our working term is cognitive coupling: two participants gradually develop shared references, correction habits and decision rules. “Entanglement” is used only as an inspiration for studying correlated coordination—not as a claim about quantum states in minds or software.

我们采用的工作术语是认知耦合：两个参与者逐渐形成共享参照、纠错习惯与决策规则。“纠缠”在这里仅用于启发对关联协同的研究，并不是关于人脑或软件中量子状态的主张。

Le bénéfice possible est une résolution plus rapide et cohérente. Le risque correspondant compte autant : un binôme familier peut renforcer les mêmes hypothèses et s’accorder trop facilement.

El posible beneficio es resolver problemas con mayor rapidez y coherencia. El riesgo es igual de importante: una pareja familiar puede reforzar los mismos supuestos y coincidir con demasiada facilidad.

The potential benefit is faster, more coherent problem solving. The matching risk is equally important: a familiar pair may reinforce the same assumptions and agree too easily.

潜在收益是更快、更连贯地解决问题；相应的风险同样重要：熟悉的搭档可能相互强化同一套假设，并过于轻易地达成一致。

02 · SAMPLE 001

Warehouse OS: a retrospective human–AI case.

The first sample is the process through which a human operator and a familiar AI collaborator developed Warehouse OS. We examine the artifacts, corrections and operational rules left by that process; this is not a controlled experiment.

HUMAN

Operational reality

Goals, constraints, field observations, corrections and final judgment.

AI

Structure and synthesis

Design proposals, implementation support, documentation and error analysis.

JOINT

Correction loop

Observe → propose → test → reject or revise → encode a durable rule.

EVIDENCE

Case evidence

Project artifacts and retrospective reconstruction; sample size one.

03 · WHAT WE OBSERVED

The useful unit may be the correction loop.

Continuity appeared to reduce repeated explanation and helped turn earlier failures into reusable constraints. The value did not come from permanent agreement; it came from the ability to remember why a proposal had failed and to revise the shared model.

That suggests an initial hypothesis: coordination quality may depend less on similarity of answers than on the quality, traceability and survival of disagreement.

04 · WHAT FAILED

Failures are part of the sample, not footnotes.

Inventory state

Ambiguous state models exposed the need for one operational source of truth.

False availability

Optimistic inference could present stock as available when the evidence did not justify it.

AI Scan

Recognition output required validation and workflow context before it could drive action.

Night camera context

Dark imagery showed that perception without environmental context can become confidently misleading.

Version integrity

A correct change is not operationally real until the intended version is identifiable and released.

Agreement risk

Shared history can accelerate work, but may also preserve shared blind spots.

05 · LIMITS

This sample cannot establish causation.

It cannot show that a familiar AI is generally better than an unfamiliar one, isolate familiarity from accumulated context and project time, establish a universal law of human–AI collaboration, or demonstrate physical quantum entanglement. Its purpose is to generate testable variables and a more disciplined second study.

06 · SAMPLE 002 PROTOCOL

Introduce an independent third-party AI.

1

Lock the task

Define one bounded Warehouse OS problem, inputs and scoring rubric before any model responds.

2

Run the familiar pair

Record solution quality, time, clarifications, corrections and confidence.

3

Run a fresh AI

Give an independent AI the same task without the accumulated relationship history.

4

Cross-critique

Compare blind spots, disagreements, revision quality and final operational value.

Current status: protocol proposed; Sample 002 has not yet been run.
