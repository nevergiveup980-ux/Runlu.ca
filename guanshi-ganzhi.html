Stems & Branches · The Grammar of Time

Time code first · interpretations separated · added signal tested

Ten stems and twelve branches combine into a repeating sixty-position index.

GUANSHI treats the calendar/indexing function as historical structure. Later elemental, zodiacal, personality and fate claims are recorded separately and earn no predictive weight without prospective evidence.

十天干与十二地支配合，形成六十位循环时间索引。

观势先把它作为历史上的历法与记时结构来研究；后来的五行、生肖、性格、命运与吉凶解释全部另账记录，没有事前验证就不给预测权重。

Dix troncs et douze branches forment un index cyclique de soixante positions.

GUANSHI traite d’abord cette fonction comme structure historique de datation. Les interprétations ultérieures — éléments, zodiaque, personnalité ou destin — restent séparées sans poids prédictif sans validation prospective.

Diez troncos y doce ramas forman un índice cíclico de sesenta posiciones.

GUANSHI trata primero esta función como estructura histórica de datación. Las interpretaciones posteriores — elementos, zodiaco, personalidad o destino — se mantienen separadas sin peso predictivo sin validación prospectiva.

02

Four ledgers

Do not mix calendar facts with symbolic claims.

1 · Dating / indexing

Historical use of stem and branch sequences to name days and later years.

2 · Calendrical correspondences

Branches became associated with months, double-hours and other calendar positions.

3 · Correlative symbolism

Later systems attached many symbolic correspondences.

4 · Predictive claims

Predictive claims require preregistered testing against sensible baselines.
