Privacy

RUNLU LIST Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RUNLU LIST for iPhone (the “App”) handles information.

1. Data collection

RUNLU LIST does not collect data from the App. The App does not transmit your list entries, quantities, completion state, Memory history, or autocomplete history to RUNLU or to third-party partners.

2. Local storage

Core App data is stored locally on your device so the App can remember your lists and previously entered items. No RUNLU account or cloud account is required to use the App.

3. Accounts, advertising, analytics and tracking

The App does not require sign-in, does not contain third-party advertising, and is not designed to use analytics or tracking SDKs that collect App data for RUNLU or third parties.

4. Device permissions

The current version of RUNLU LIST does not require access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or advertising identifier for its core features.

5. Purchases

If RUNLU LIST is obtained as a paid App Store app, Apple processes the purchase. RUNLU does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

6. Support communications

If you contact RUNLU by email, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your support request. Support email is separate from data handled inside the App.

7. Data deletion

Because core App data is stored locally, you can remove list content using App controls. Deleting the App from your device may also remove its locally stored App data, subject to iOS behavior and any device backup you control.

8. Children

RUNLU LIST is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not directed specifically to children.

9. Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about RUNLU LIST, contact support@runlu.ca.
