RUNLU GUANSHI · V0.6 · T03

Five Phases · The Grammar of Systems

Functions first · relations mapped · feedback tested

Wuxing is more useful to GUANSHI as a language of interacting processes than as a list of five substances.

The question becomes: what supports what, what constrains what, where regulation fails, and which feedback loop is changing the system?

在观势里，五行更适合作为“五种相互作用过程”的语言，而不是五种神秘物质的清单。

真正的问题是：谁在支持谁？谁在约束谁？制衡在哪里失效？是哪一条反馈回路正在改变整个系统？

Pour GUANSHI, le wuxing est plus utile comme langage de processus interactifs que comme liste de cinq substances.

La question devient : qu’est-ce qui soutient quoi, qu’est-ce qui contraint quoi, où la régulation échoue-t-elle, et quelle boucle de rétroaction transforme le système ?

Para GUANSHI, el wuxing es más útil como lenguaje de procesos interactivos que como lista de cinco sustancias.

La pregunta pasa a ser: ¿qué sostiene a qué, qué limita a qué, dónde falla la regulación y qué bucle de retroalimentación está cambiando el sistema?

Five Phases is a traditional process model, not a scientifically established set of natural forces.

01

Historical correction

Early Wuxing is not the whole later correspondence system.

Early textual anchor

The Hongfan chapter of the Shangshu is a major early source.

Later system building

Across later periods, Five-Phase categories were integrated with many other systems.

02

Five process images

Treat the names as functional images before treating them as correspondences.

水 · Water

Flow / descent

An early image of moistening and moving downward.

火 · Fire

Rise / activation

An image of flaming upward.

木 · Wood

Growth / flexibility

The old image emphasizes bending and straightening.

金 · Metal

Form / transformation

The early description centers on conforming and changing.

土 · Earth

Cultivation / mediation

The early image is cultivation and crop production.

The order and meanings found in an early text should not be silently replaced by a later diagram.

03

Six operating relations

From five labels to a network of support, constraint and feedback.

01 · 生

Support / generation

Traditional sheng relationships suggest one process enabling another.

02 · 克

Constraint / overcoming

Traditional ke relationships suggest limitation.

03 · 制

Regulation

Support without constraint can overshoot; constraint without support can stall.

04 · 乘

Overconstraint

Later technical traditions use vocabulary for excessive domination.

05 · 侮

Counter-reaction

Later traditions also describe reversal of an expected constraint.

06 · 环

Cycle / feedback

When several links close into a loop, the effect may amplify, stabilize, oscillate or arrive with delay.

04

Translation into system dynamics

Symbol → variable → link → loop → data → revision.

1 · Name the systemWhat are we actually studying?
2 · Name variablesReplace symbolic labels with observables
3 · Draw linksSupport, constrain, delay
4 · Close loopsReinforcing or balancing?
5 · MeasureBaseline, trend, lag, outcome
6 · ReviseKeep only what improves explanation or prediction
This translation does not claim that Wuxing and modern system dynamics are the same theory.

05

Practical translation examples

Never stop at “this belongs to Wood” or “that overcomes Fire.”

Business / project

Define real variables first.

Personal decision

A symbolic support relation becomes concrete only after variables are specified.

06

Evidence boundary

Use the pattern as a hypothesis generator, never as automatic proof.

GUANSHI may keep
  • multi-variable relational thinking
  • support and constraint as separate link types
  • attention to cycles, thresholds and rebound
  • the idea that regulation may fail by excess or insufficiency
GUANSHI does not assume
  • five literal invisible forces govern every event
  • traditional correspondence tables are causal laws
  • sheng/ke sequences predict outcomes without data
  • a scientific term can be borrowed to validate a traditional claim

07

Source anchors
