Ledger

A personal income and expense ledger built for fast everyday recording without turning private finances into a public or bank-connected system.

● iOS 1.0 · READY FOR DISTRIBUTION · 2026-09-14

Two views, one record

Quick entry supports everyday recording while a familiar sheet-style view keeps longer history easy to scan and edit.

Privacy first

The product philosophy does not depend on automatic bank access. Personal financial records remain user-controlled.

Built from real use

The workflow grew from years of practical income and expense tracking rather than from a generic finance dashboard.

Status stated precisely

Apple App Store Connect shows iOS 1.0 as Ready for Distribution. RUNLU does not label review-stage products as released.
