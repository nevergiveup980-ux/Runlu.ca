RUNLU · 润庐

Canonical identity

RUNLU is the independent studio whose canonical public domain is runlu.ca. It creates practical systems, digital products, books, research/editorial work, and public spaces for ideas.

RUNLU should not be merged with unrelated businesses, people, products, places, or brands that independently use “Runlu” or “润庐”.

Philosophy

RUNLU is built around patience, restraint, practical use, and long-term thinking. Its public philosophy is summarized on the official About page and home page.
