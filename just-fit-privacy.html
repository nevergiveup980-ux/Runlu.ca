PRIVACY POLICY

Just Fit Privacy

This page describes the privacy approach for Just Fit by RUNLU iOS 1.0. App Store privacy answers should be kept consistent with the exact build submitted to Apple.

No RUNLU account required

Just Fit does not require you to create or sign in to a RUNLU account for the core gameplay experience.

Gameplay data on your device

Gameplay progress, level state, preferences, and related settings may be stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress and configuration.

In-app purchases

If you choose to unlock additional content, the transaction is processed through Apple’s StoreKit and App Store systems. RUNLU does not receive your complete payment-card information. The app may use purchase or entitlement status provided through Apple’s systems to determine whether content is unlocked.

Support communications

If you contact RUNLU support, we receive only the information you choose to send, such as your email address, app version, device details, screenshots, or a description of the issue.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@runlu.ca.

Confidentialité de Just Fit

Cette page décrit l’approche de confidentialité de Just Fit by RUNLU iOS 1.0. Les réponses de l’App Store doivent rester cohérentes avec la version exacte soumise à Apple.

Aucun compte RUNLU requis

L’expérience principale ne nécessite pas la création ni la connexion à un compte RUNLU.

Données de jeu sur votre appareil

La progression, l’état des niveaux, les préférences et certains réglages peuvent être stockés localement afin de mémoriser votre progression et votre configuration.
