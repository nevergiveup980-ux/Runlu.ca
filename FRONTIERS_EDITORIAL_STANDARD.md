# RUNLU FRONTIERS — Editorial Standard

Effective: 12 September 2026

RUNLU FRONTIERS is an original perspective series built from public research signals, technical developments and field evidence. It is not a clipping, translation or rewriting column.

## Core rule

Every FRONTIERS piece must clearly separate three layers:

1. **Research Signal** — what public research, projects, papers or real-world developments actually show. Cite direct sources and state important limits.
2. **RUNLU View** — RUNLU's independent analysis, judgment or interpretation derived from those signals.
3. **RUNLU Concept** — RUNLU's forward-looking hypothesis, framework or design direction. These must be labelled as proposals or concepts rather than established facts.

## Required opening disclosure

Every FRONTIERS article should carry a concise, visible disclosure near the title:

> **RUNLU Original Perspective** — This article is not an excerpt, translation or rewrite of an existing article or research paper. It is an independent RUNLU analysis built from publicly available research and recent technical developments. Research facts are sourced; interpretations, judgments and forward-looking concepts are identified as RUNLU's own.

Localized versions should preserve the same meaning in English, Chinese, French and Spanish.

Chinese standard wording:

> **RUNLU 原创观点** — 本文并非对现有文章或研究的摘抄、翻译或改写，而是基于公开研究资料与近期技术进展，由 RUNLU 独立形成的分析、判断与前瞻性构想。文中研究事实注明来源；分析、判断与前瞻构想明确标识为 RUNLU 自身观点。

## Required closing boundary

Every FRONTIERS article must end with direct sources and an **Evidence Boundary** explaining which statements are supported by cited work and which ideas are RUNLU interpretations or concepts.

## Editorial discipline

- Do not manufacture novelty claims.
- Do not call a concept scientifically proven unless the evidence supports that statement.
- Do not hide source dependence: strong original thinking begins with accurate attribution.
- Prefer primary research and first-party technical disclosures, supplemented by independent reporting when useful.
- Distinguish implementation, inference and speculation.
- A FRONTIERS number is earned by an original RUNLU judgment, not by the existence of a news event.

## Series principle

**RUNLU does not copy conclusions. RUNLU studies public evidence, connects signals, and forms its own judgment.**

This standard applies to FRONTIERS 001 and all future RUNLU FRONTIERS editions.
