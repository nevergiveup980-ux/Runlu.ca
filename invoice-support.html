Universal Invoice Support

For help with RUNLU Universal Invoice, send us a short description of what happened. Including a few details makes troubleshooting much faster.

When contacting support

  • App version and build number
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you were trying to do
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain information you do not want to share

Subscriptions

If the subscription is not recognized after reinstalling or changing devices, use the app's Restore Purchases entry while signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Billing and App Store charges

Subscription billing is handled by Apple. Account-level billing issues, refunds, and App Store payment methods may need to be managed through Apple's App Store services.

Document export and email

If exporting or sharing fails, include the export format and the destination you selected. Email handoff also depends on the email capabilities configured on the device.
